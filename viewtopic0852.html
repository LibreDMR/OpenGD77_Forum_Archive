Post by F1RMB » Tue Jan 28, 2025 6:30 pm

Also, if you press three time on the # key, you can have access to the whole contact list.
The OpenGD77 firmware is more designed to have a channel per repeater, one contact list linked to it, then you can change the current TS, type the TG/PC ID, or select any contact from the codeplug contacts.

Of course, this is not mandatory, but it makes the life much easier, in opposite of the "commercial" DMR way (on channel per contact, per TG/PC: a total nightmare...).

Yes, that's right. Radio can hear everything what is on repeater or hotspot, if you have dmr/ts filters turned off etc. All this you can set in a quick menu from the radio.
You don't have to programm these groups in codeplug. It is the advantage of OpenGD77, is much simpler.
In addition, if you want to TX on some group that you will not have programmed, you can simply enter its number on the keyboard (after pressing #) and connect to it. The same with private contact with the difference that you press # x2.
