Post by G4EML » Fri Sep 20, 2024 6:59 pm

That's good news. The markings on the board can't really be relied upon. Our definition of V2, V4 , V5 is only something we invented for our own use. The manufacturers don't seem to identify the different versions.

The differences are in the synthesiser chips and the IF chips, so only the correct version will work.

If the radio receives and transmits then you must be using the correct version.

Colin G4EML
