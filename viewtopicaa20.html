This question has been asked many times before on this forum.

Neither TYT or Baofeng seems to bother to calibrate these radios very accurately.

Try to calibrate your Hotspot to work with your OpenGD77 radio.

Alternatively calibrate your OpenGD77 radio to your other 2 radios.

Calibration functionality is documented in the User Guide
