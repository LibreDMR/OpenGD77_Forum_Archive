Thanks to Alex DL4LEX..

We now have a way to add new Contacts in the OpenGD77 firmware

There is a new menu item for Contacts with options of "Contact list" and "New contact"

Both TalkGroup and Private Call contacts can be added.

In the Contacts list, the initial display are the TG's . To access the Private Calls, press the # key

Access to the Contacts list is also available from the CHannel and VFO screens by pressing Function (Blue) + #, and press # to get access the PC list.

I've uploaded these new features into the OpenGD77_latest.sgl

https://github.com/rogerclarkmelbourne/ ... latest.sgl

Specifically this commit to github

https://github.com/rogerclarkmelbourne/ ... latest.sgl


Note.
Although Alex and I have tested this. There is a slight chance it may have bugs, including corrupting your codeplug in the radio
