* The Chinese font library and the pinyin library are moved into the last 512K of external FLASH in the new version, taking up part of the space of the original GPS LOG (originally occupied 2M, now reduced to 1.5M)*

Note: 1) Flash the XXXXX write Chinese table.zip in order, wait for the screen to display and turn off, and then shut down;
2) The second step is to burn the firmware of the Pinyin library (XXXXX write pinyin map.zip), and then shut down after the screen display is completed and turned off.
3) Burn the latest OpenXXXXX_Chinese Rxxxxxxx.zip firmware and shut it down.
4) Press and hold the SK1 number button "5", turn on the computer at the same time, the screen will display "GPS record clearing..", wait for completion.

* The above steps of writing to the font and pinyin only need to be performed once, and the subsequent firmware update does not need to be carried out again. *

@ Added MDC RogerTone option
Sound Settings -->>Roger tone: off/MDC-1/MDC-2, 3 options,

@MDC ID settings
Display Settings --?MDC ID: 0000, Left arrow key to select position, right arrow key to change number (0~F)

@MDC Call Alert
Press the # key to enter the DTMF input, press the number keys 0~9, the left arrow key="A", the up key="B", the down key="C", the right arrow key="D", * = "E", # = "F", after entering the 4-digit ID of the other party, press and hold the “SK2” + GREEN key, the screen will display "MCD CALL", and the transceiver will automatically send MDC CALL signaling

@ TYT MD-UV390 PLUS 10W model has different hardware versions, if you find that the backlight is not bright, please choose one in OpenMDUV380_10W_PLUS_Chinese.bin or OpenMDUV390_10W_PLUS_Chinese.bin, the normal backlight is the correct firmware
