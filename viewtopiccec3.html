As far as we know all versions and variants of the MD9600 are the same. The volume control is a linear law pot not a log law pot as it should be.

The only real fix is to replace the pot with the correct type. The manufacturers don't seem to be willing to do this. Possibly aa a cost saving exercise.

Post by G4EML » Sun Aug 18, 2024 9:15 pm

That resistor is the factory mod to improve the volume control. It is an old trick to change the slope of a potentiometer from linear to something closer to log law.

I originally suggested the mod with a 1K resistor but the factory decided to change that to 2K2. I still think 1K works better.

If you think it is bad now you should have tried it before they started fitting the mod!

I am still surprised that they have never changed to the proper log law pot. I can only assume it is a component supply or cost saving issue.
