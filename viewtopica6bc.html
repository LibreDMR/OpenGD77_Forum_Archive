OK, I got you now. OpenGD77 is working in a different way than commercial/traditional DMR. In OpenGD77 the top line is talk group and the bottom line is channel.

on 1801 radio, you change channel by arrow up/down and change talkgroup with left/right
how to write a codeplug is the key. In Traditional DMR you will have to create multiple channel for a repeater. These channels are almost identical, only talk group and sometimes time slot that changed. on the radio, you can only change channels, there is noway to change talkgroup, except manual dial.

In OpenGD77, we use only one channel per repeater. then create a "talkgroup list" and attach the talkgroup list to the channel. Now on the radio you can change talkgroup.

Hope this make sense
