I have successfully put GP-02 module into my DM-1701. I decided to use the module, not kit, without backup battery. I might add a battery in the future, but the GPS cold boot takes only less than a minute, so I'm satisfied for now.

The N/F pin on the module is function perfectly. on a bench top power supply the module takes 18 mA when N/F is high and 0 mA when N/F is low. So I decided not to use N-MOS.

wiring details
3V3 (RED) <--> PIN 8 of Flash memory chip
GND (BLACK) <--> PIN 4 of Flash memory chip
N/F (GREEN) <--> Pin 8 (PA9) on the left side counting from the bottom of the STM32 chip
TX (YELLOW) <--> Pin 7 (PA10) on the left side counting from the bottom of the STM32 chip
