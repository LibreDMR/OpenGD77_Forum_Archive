The OpenGD77 firmware is completely different to the official firmware on any of these radios.
The OpenGD77/DM1801/RD5R does not have a lot of the features that are in the official firmware.
SMS is not supported, nor is DCDM and various other things.

Your guess is as good as mine to why various features are not in the official RD5R firmware, but ultimately the firmware for all these radios is written by the same secretive organistion, whose name we can't determine.

The OpenRD5R is most of the features of the OpenGD77/ OpenDM108, except for Hotspot mode, and some features in the future can't be supported on the RD-5R due to hardware limitations.
