Post by DC8OH » Sun May 18, 2025 6:55 pm

It works now.
My fault, i connected pin 99+100 of cpu for easier soldering, after only connecting pin 99 it is ok.
Now the 3d printer has to print a little hat...

CAUTION: Do not use the power of torch led for powering gps module, it is plus of battery!

The switched cpu port from the torch led can not be used direct for switching power of the gps module without desoldering the transister because the basis emitter diode of the transistor limits the voltage to about 0.7 volt.

Christian

Update 05-31-2025
Finally i connected GND of the gps modulde to the collector of the led transistor to be able to switch power of the gps module.
After powercycle it is always needed to switch the gps to on with "SK2 + 5", if it is needed.

Disadvantage
There is no possibility to check if the powerstatus of the gps is on or off.
