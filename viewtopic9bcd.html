It could be that the frequency offset between the radio and hotspot is too large , do you use the hotspot with several DMR radios ?
Then it's a bit more complicated as all radios and the hotspot must be on the same frequency , even a few 100 Hz difference can be bad.
I think DMR specifies a frequency accuracy of 0.5 ppm or better.

I use a frequency counter and adjusted all my radios and my hotspots as close as possible to the configured frequency, then I also get a very low bit error on the hotspot.

There are other ways to adjust all of the equipment to use the same freqyency , using a RTL-SDR or a VHF/UHF rig with SSB/CW to zero beat them on the same carrier frequency.

Or you can try to use the adjustments on the hotspot , pi-star has a calibrate function in the web gui and pistar-mmdvmcal via the cli.


Another cause could be that you have different CCS/DMR ID on the radio compared to the hotspot and using private mode.
