So I have the standard charger that comes with a GD-77. I noticed the battery wasn't quite hanging in there like it used to (it's old) but also that the charger would turn red during charging but then after a long while start flashing between red and green. If I leave it alone long enough, it went green, but I figured this must be a sign that my aging battery was on the way out.

Purchased a brand new replacement battery from Radioddity, but now that battery on its first charge is doing the same thing!

Is the red/green flashing one of those 90% charged indicators that some Motorola chargers do or is my charger broken?
