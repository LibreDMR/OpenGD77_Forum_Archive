VK2VL wrote: ↑
Sat Apr 19, 2025 7:37 am
 Where can I get an older cps so I can at least establish communication.
We don't keep an archive of all the old copies of the CPS.

You could try archive.org to see if that site took a backup, at the time of the firmware version you are using.

However, your better option would simply be to update the firmware to the latest Beta.

The firmware update is not via the COM port, so it doesn't matter whether the COM port works or not.
