Post by G4EML » Fri Sep 15, 2023 5:08 pm

Open GD77 CPS searches for the port by name, not number. So it should find the radio on any port number. However it sounds like on your computer it is not able to reliably identify the port.

The fact that windows has allocated COM25 to the port might indicate that you have previously used other serial port devices on that computer. It may be that the drivers for those devices, even if they are not plugged in, are causing the problem. Similar problems have benn reported by a few people before.

It might be worth manually re-assigning the com port to a different (lower) number. You can do that in the advanced settings when looking at the com port properties in device manager.
It may say that ports are in use even though the hardware for them is not currently connected. You can normally safely re-use those ports if you are sure the hardware is not present.
