RD-5R - cannot connect to hotspot - Timeout

  • Quote

Post by DL6EK » Mon May 05, 2025 6:04 am

Hello,
I got an RD-5R second hand, installation of OGD77 went fine, thanks to all the devs.

The issue I have is that the radio cannot 'reach/activate'' the hotspot, error message: Timeout.
Two other radios Anytone 878 and DM1701 (OGD77) can activate and communicate through hotspot.
RD-5R can communicate via a DMR relais (Hytera RD625), outside the reach from where I'm located.

What I've checked with the RD-5R:
  • Tried two different MMDVM duplex boards on two different RPis
    both running latest Pi-Star 4.2.3 with latest HS Hat fw 1.6.1
    no change with duplex/simplex config
  • Both OGD77 releases showing same issue (R20240908 and Beta20250323)
  • Frequency deviation seems not the issue, tested with TinySA and SDR
  • Calibration seems to be fine (compared with a ham providing screenshots)
Any more hints to troubleshoot the issue?

Post by DL6EK » Tue May 06, 2025 4:55 pm

Thanks for the suggestions!
Finally it was an issue with the calibration data.
After firmware installation and backup of calibration data the reference oscillator tuning was originally -55.
Changing it to -41 I was able to activate the mmdvm hotspot, with fine tuning to -37 I'm happy with very low BER.
Usually I try to avoid mess with calibration data, in this case with the second hand device I'm good.
