Baofeng DM-1701; receives DMR; transmit DMR fails

  • Quote

Post by N0NV » Wed Jan 15, 2025 12:47 am

My DM-1701 receives DMR talkgroups from a WPSD hotspot fine. Transmit to the hotspot (and up into the talkgroup) fails. The HT had the same problem on the OEM firmware, and I had hoped upgrading to OpenGD77 might fix it.
Today I am tinkering with the calibration settings on the radio screen. Likely my last attempt with the radio.
Over weeks I adjusted the hotspot - its BER and signal adjustments, frequency assignment, various antennas, etc. I am confident in my hotspot configs, which are validated by separate success on Alinco and Yaesu stock HTs.
The DM-1701 currently Rx/Tx no problem on FM repeaters and simplex. Monitoring a kerchunk to the hotspot on FM set to the simplex freq - I can hear a helicopter blade thumping sounding hiss. The DM-1701 is resulting in a Tx, but no intelligible signal to the hotspot.

Has anyone seen this problem before? And how to fix?

At this point, I am fine tossing the DM-1701 on the shelf of analog HTs. However, based on the community activity and documentation, I know people are getting these radios running.

Most counters only seem to resolve to 1kHz, which isn't good enough when calibrating multiple radios to work with the same hotspot, as the Hotspot Rx window is only around +/- 200Hz at most.
