Only a proposal for GD-77S improvement

Hello,

recently I got a GD-77S. I flashed OpenGD77 firmware and prepared a seperate codeplug. Thank you very much for the implementation of OpenGD77 firmware! Very amazing... But I found it was hard to program because there is no TG-list available. So all frequencies have to be programmed with an individual talkgroup (like the chinese firmware/CPSs). With adding a TG-list as an extra control menu you could choose a frequency within a zone and after selecting the TG-list menu you could step through the TGs with the rotary switch. That would be similar to the devices with a display and would be very helpful on all DMR repeater frequencies. Perhaps that could be implemented somewhere in the future if time allows it.

Thank you very much again for your excellent work!
73s de DF1OG, Ingo
