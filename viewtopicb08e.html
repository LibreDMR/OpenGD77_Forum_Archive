I have mod my RD-5R charger and set the charging current to 1 A (RTS = 0.1 Ohm). Trying with the original power supply (10V 0.5A) the battery was being charged at 0.6A. When switched to a 12V 3A power supply, the battery was being charged at 1 A. I then switched back to original power supply and completed the charge on the test battery. Everything was well, the power supply is in the normal temp.

In conclusion, If the selected charging current is more than the power supply can provide, the module will charge at maximum current it can, nothing damage.
