I'm not sure if this is a bug or rather a limitation of an internal measurement in the radio, but I've noticed that the battery voltage percentage (upper right of main display) fluctuates quite a bit. It will increase sometimes after having a QSO. I'm assuming this is because a real time voltage measurement changes before/during/after talking. If that is the case, maybe a time averaging of measurements (over say 180 s) might help level this out?

Re: Battery voltage percentage

  • Quote

Post by VK3KYY » Sun Nov 17, 2019 7:53 pm

It’s a rolling 1 minute average sampled once per second.

The reason why the level is changing is because the battery voltage really is going down and up again.

I guess I could increase the averaging period. But if you turn it off and on again, the average will be lost and the value could change a lot between the value you saw prior to turning it off and the value you see when you turn in on again
