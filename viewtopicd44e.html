Someone emailed me links to YT videos about this today.

Looks like Anytone copied all of the OpenGD77 satellite functionality

Prediction on the Anytone seems to be very very slow, I was also told that sometimes its incorrect.
They also can't spell "predicting", adding an extra "i" into their version of this word

The OpenGD77 predictions have been verified by many people against the AMSAT web page predictions and also against various apps, and have always been correct.

Check the Anytone predictions against the AMSAT website etc

Also be aware that the predictions are only as good as the keps data. Do you know where Anytone sources their keps ?
With OpenGD77 you can specify from where you download the keps.
The default is https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/amateur.txt because normally this is the most accurate source.

PS.

I'm not an expert in satellite operation, but this functionality is tested by people who are very expert in this.
