They stopped making the RD5R over a year ago.

This was initially because of supply problems with the CPU during the COVID epidemic, and even now I think the CPU is not easy to source so manufacturers gave up on all radios which use the NXP Freescale MK22 CPU.
The same applies to the GD77 and DM1801

So the only radios you will find for sale are either very old stock or second hand
