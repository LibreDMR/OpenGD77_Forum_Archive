You can step down to frequency calibration with SK2+Down Arrow when you are in the calibration menu.

I have used my frequency counter and adjusted all radios and hotspots to be as accurate as possible.
Bit error is less than 0.2% in pi-star nodes.

You can also use a SDR with VHF/UHF coverage and at least adjust all your equipment to use the same carrier frequency.

The first submenu is for power calibration on a number of frequencies.
For frequency calibration you have two calibration points one for VHF and one for uhf.
Use sk2+down arrow to jump down to the frequency menus.
You must use sk2 +green to save changes to nvram
