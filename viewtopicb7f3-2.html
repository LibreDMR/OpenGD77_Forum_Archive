I have been reviewing the thread on the MD-UV380 / RT3S GPS upgrade or addition, plus the one thread on MD-9600 new antenna install.

I am curious if there is anyone who has installed GPS into MD-9600 that did not come originally with GPS. Also it has been mentioned in the past about adding a 3.5 mm plug on the MD-9600 for an external GPS antenna.

Any update or recent information on either of these two queries ?

I have several MD-9600 radios and with the APRS work and satellite function it seems have a good GPS would be most helpful.

Post by VK3KYY » Sun Nov 12, 2023 9:08 am

I have an external GPS attached to my MD-9600 via a cable from the back of the radio.

This cable is connected to the GPS connections in the front panel PCB of the radio and runs over the top of the PCB and out of the back of the radio through a hole in the heat sink.

V4A aka V5 hardware radios have a rubber plug in the heat sink, which can be removed, and the cable can be passed through he resulting hole.
