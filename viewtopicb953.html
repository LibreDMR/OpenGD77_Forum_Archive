I just checked and I'm pretty sure its because the radio was turned off before entering DFU mode

i.e If you turn off the radio from the green button on the front panel
Then turn off the PSU
Then hold Orange and P1 to enter DFU , and while still holding Orange and P1, turn on the PSU... The radio will power on and enter DFU mode
Then load the firmware
The radio will now reboot, but becuase the previous state prior to turning on the PSU with Orange and P1 held in.... Was OFF

Then the firmware will be in its OFF state


The confusion is proabbly becuase this radio is never actually OFF.

There is no power control circuity in this radio. When the radio appears to be off, its only every actually in Standby. The CPU is still running, albeit in a low current standby mode.

This is the same as the official fimware

You can tell that the radio is never truely off, even with the official firmware, becuase if you measure the current when the radio is "OFF" you'll find its not 0 (zero)
I forget how much current the radio takes when its in standby, its a few milliamps, possibly 10 or 15mA , I can't remember the exact value.

Post by W3ATN » Sun Mar 24, 2024 12:33 am

I figured out the problem. However, first I need to clarify what the problem was. It wasn't an FW issue. It had to do with the CPS writing the CP to the radio.

There was a coincidence of events that made the shut down occur. I got the latest CPS software and used it AFTER I'd changed a setting on the radio to "Safe Pwr-On". What that does is require you to hold the SK1 button (above PTT) while powering on the radio. This prevents accidental (e.g., in a backpack) turning on of the unit.

If you watch the UV-890 screen when you write a CP to it, the radio flashes off/on at the end of the write process. I turned OFF the "Safe Pwr-On" mode and the new CPS behaves normally - after writing to the radio, the radio flashes off then immediately back on. Turn on the "Safe Pwr-On" and it can't because you're not holding that SK1 key when it tries to turn on.

So, nothing wrong with CPS, just a quirk caused by a radio setting.

I can confirm that it is the "Safe Pwr-on" option that triggers this behaviour. I noticed this happening a couple of weeks ago, but eventually decided it wasn't an unreasonable side effect of selecting "Safe Pwr-on" (I've only just started using this option), so I didn't bother raising it as a bug.

As stated by W3ATH, the clue is that if you watch carefully you can see a flash of life as the radio begins the reboot, and then it encounters the Safe Pwr-on flag and quits the boot process.

This seems sensible to me as it preserves, and is in compliance with, the settings that were present prior to whatever update has just been made.
