SO....for those (still) looking at this thread; here's what to take-away:

- Adding your ID to every channel makes for a messy codeplug.
- Be sure to double-check which DMR ID you enter in the File Menu -> Settings -> DMR ID and callsign. (The callsign is NOT what is transmitted to the Network; just your ID (number).)

1) DMR ID and callsign: Enter my callsign and Radio (Digital) ID

2) Contacts -> Digital Contacts: I added additional TalkGroups that I use

3) TG Lists: I modified 1 of your analog and 6 of your digital lists
