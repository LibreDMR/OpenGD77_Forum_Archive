Great.
Well, if you notice some weird value (like crazy ColorCodem, Power, FM channel becoming DMR by their own, CTCSS/DCS self enabling value, unwanted boot image/melody changes and stuff like that), you can suspect codeplug corruption.
On the CPS side, after reading the codeplug from the transceiver, and noticing weird values in channels, or unmanageable zones, you can suspect some corruption.
It's always good to store codeplug evolutions stages, as you can go back before the corruption. Another one is to export and store the CSV files generated by the CPS, as it's fast and easy to rebuild a codeplug from scratch.

Cheers.
