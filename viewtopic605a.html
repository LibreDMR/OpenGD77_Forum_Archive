Hi all, an update and big thank you to Daniel KK7OSL who got on a Zoom call with me tonight and got me unstuck. Daniel had me get on the Bellevue/Cougar Mtn repeater (N7ERP) and try the parrot while monitoring the live monitor of connecting stations (https://pnwdigital.net/bridgewatch/?filter=PNW) and it turned out the time slot was locked (see picture) even though when I went to the menu it showed the correct time slot. I'm still not sure why that happened. The TS graphic color on the screen is inverted presumably indicating that it's locked. I'll dig into the users manual to see if I can find it but if anyone knows, I'd love to know.
I'm still not getting anything from the parrot on W7ACS (441.025+,CC2, TS1) even though the CC and TS were correct. Though Doug Kingston KD7DK, one of our local DMR (and all things ham) elmers said that the W7ACS repeater had unreliable performance, so that's perhaps it.
I'll play around more on the weekend, but at least I'm hearing audio from the N7ERP repeater (both the parrot and Daniel got me tuned in to here some conversations on TG 3100). Thanks much again to KK7OLS for the help! And to everyone else for the replies and suggestions!
Christian K7XTN
