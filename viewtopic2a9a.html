I have a V2 and it works quite good , the "Click Suppression" takes away all annoying clicks.
The only problem I have is that some of the SVXLink repeaters here in Stockholm seems to be wider than my radio even if I select wide on the
radio.
So I notice some severe clipping on RX on received TX.

I've also seen on my deviation meter that the deviation is around 4 kHZ even in Wide setting on TX , I haven't tried yet with my signal generator
to feed wider signal to see if I can reproduce what I hear on live traffic.

I have some ideas to change the software to use the widest setting on the circuit that handles FM RX so I can see if that makes any difference.
The IC have three different bandwidths and the widest is around 7 kHz.
