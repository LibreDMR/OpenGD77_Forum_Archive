Hi everyone,

In my opinion, the ideal path forward would be implementing a proper AFSK and APRS transcoding solution that works reliably in both transmit and receive modes. This would ensure that APRS data is consistently interpreted, no matter which radio or software is involved.

Additionally, there are two key “missing pieces” that would really enhance APRS functionality:

Analog APRS Reception – being able to monitor APRS beacons natively on analog, not just transmit.
SMS Handling – support for sending and receiving text messages via both APRS and DMR.
Having these features in one integrated firmware (like OpenGD77, if it’s ever planned) would be a huge step forward for those of us who rely on APRS over various protocols—especially in regions where we need local point-to-point APRS because we’re too far from official gateways.

Hi everyone,

I've just tested the APRS transmission from my RT-3S w/ OpenGD77 to my Anytone878.
You need to switch the OpenGD77 APRS compression "Off" to enable the correct GPS coordinates on the Anytone.
With APRS compression "On" the Anytone shows a nonsense GPS location.

I've also checked my RT-3S APRS function with a local Digipeater. It successfully decodes the GPS coordinates independent of compression "On" or "Off".

Best regards
