The CPS currently does not support lists of regions

You can however download data for 505 and then download data for anothe region, and the other region(s) will be appended

The subsequent presses on Download don't actually download again, it uses the cached download, but it still takes time to filter based on the region and append those DMR ID's to the master list that is compressed and uploaded to the radio when you press the Write button
