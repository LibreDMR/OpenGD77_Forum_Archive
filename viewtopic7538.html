Post by VK3KYY » Wed Jun 15, 2022 11:07 pm

Can someone confirm whether these new DM1801 radios are DMR ?

Someone posted that they are FM only.

Also, can you confirm whether the USB cable contains a chip in the plug / dongle end ?
You can check this by using a continuity tester to see which pins in the USB plug connect to which of the pads on the 2.5 and 3.5mm jack plugs

All modern CPU (MCU) chips, (in the last 10 years), have USB support and use direct USB connection from the PC to the radio.

If Baofeng had re-engineered this radio, they would have probably replaced the CPU and C6000 DMR Chip, with the C7000 DMR chip which also contains a CPU.
However the C7000 has built in USB support and does not use a chip in the cable.


If these radios have a firmware update mode, and you connect them to a PC, the Windows Device Manager, will show the type of device that is connected to the PC.
Unfortunately I can't remember what USB device is detected when a C7000 chip is in the radio.

The other thing you could do, is open the radio and look at what chips are inside, but I doubt anyone is willing to do this.
