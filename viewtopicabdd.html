An update on the current consumption

The Official firmware consumes 150mA with screen on and not power saving
When in power saving , with the screen on, the offial firmware seems to consume pulses of 100mA and 150mA, so average is probably 125mA

The OpenUV380 consumes about 205mA with the screen at 100%

At the moment I do not know why the OpenUV380 consumes about 50mA more than the official firmware.
Possibly it is because my radio has a GPS, and the GPS may not be enabled in the official firmware, but I don't know how much current the GPS module would consume.

I have started to work on Eco modes, but at the moment if I enable Eco, the Rx stops working.
