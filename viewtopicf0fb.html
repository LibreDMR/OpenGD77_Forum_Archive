Hello everyone, and thank you very much for joining the forum.

I'm sharing my problem to see if anyone else has had a similar experience.

I have a hotspot that I can access from any of my digital devices (TYT9600 and 878 UV2+) without any problems.

I just purchased a Retevis RT3s and installed opengd77, but I can't seem to "open" the hotspot when I press the PTT button on my new walkie-talkie. I've tried everything, including inserting the TYT9600's cocodeplug, since it works on this device, but I can't open the HS. I've also tried correcting the hotspot frequency, but to no avail. It doesn't receive anything, and as soon as I move it from its current location, the "Time Out" message starts appearing on the Retevis screen, so I assume there's no need to move it. I'm starting to think the modem is broken, but... Has anyone else had something similar happen to them...?

Thank you all so much!!

73s.
