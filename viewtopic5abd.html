BH7HQT wrote: ↑
Tue Mar 05, 2024 2:20 am
 On MD-780(MD2017 without GPS),the radio time will stop when power off.Is the built-in RTC not work?
The firmware does use the RTC.

However if the RTC battery is defective or not charged, the the RTC will not function

RTC battery is a separate small rechargable battery which is only charged when the radio is turned On.

When the radio is turned off the battery will become flat after appoximately 3 weeks.

Charging of the RTC battery is very slow. 24 hours is the normal time to fully charge the battery
