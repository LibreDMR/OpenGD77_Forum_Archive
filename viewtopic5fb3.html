ECO: The radio station does not transmit unless it is "woken up" beforehand. That is, when you press PTT with the screen turned off, everything looks as if there is a transmission, but the hotspot does not receive a signal.

PS
It's not about the burning screen. After 10-12 seconds, the station will not transmit when the PTT is pressed, unless you wake it up.

PS PS
Similar to ECO-1, the transmission is not blocked after a long wait
