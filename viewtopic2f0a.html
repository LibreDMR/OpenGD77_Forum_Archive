Post by G4EML » Wed Mar 17, 2021 12:06 am

If you are seeing the cable on its own as a USB device then it is not the correct cable for use with the GD77.

The GD77 cable has no electronics in it, so it will not be seen by the computer on its own. The radio is the USB device and is only seen by the computer when it is connected and turned on. It uses the driver included with the CPS download.

Post by F1RMB » Wed Mar 17, 2021 6:18 am

Hi,

As Roger asked, which firmware is installed on your RD-5R ?
In bootloader mode, the HT is a HID device VID:15a2, PID:0073. While in "nornal" mode, it's a COM port, VID:1fc9, PID:0094.
With the manufacturer firmware, the device is always in a HID one, and not supported by the OpenGD77 CPS.
