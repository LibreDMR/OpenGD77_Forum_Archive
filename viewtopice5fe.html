Hello everybody,
Hoping to do something nice,
I want to share with you the codeplug I created for the OpenGD77 complete with all the Italian repeaters separated by zones of all networks (BM, IT-DMR, DMR +, DMR-ITALIA) and all the Italian TGs and clusters.
Just open it with the CPS Community and enter your Call and DMR-ID, and upload it to the radio.
I hope you can enjoy it.
Greetings to the whole OpenGD77 community and a special thanks to Roger clark who allows us to do all this.
73 by IU4LEG Fabio
