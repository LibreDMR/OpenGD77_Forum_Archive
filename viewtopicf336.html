Re: version 2024 12 15 removed GPS option from MD-UV380

  • Quote

Post by G4EML » Sun Jan 05, 2025 12:48 am

All versions of the firmware test for the presence of a GPS module at power on. The GPS power is turned on and the firmware looks for NMEA messages from the module. If it does not receive anything then it assumes the GPS is not fitted and sets the GPS state to None.

So your radio is not seeing the GPS module. That could be a hardware failure, or it could be a poor connection in the ribbon cable between the PCBs. The ribbon cables are known to be a weak point so it would be worth reseating both ends of the cable.
