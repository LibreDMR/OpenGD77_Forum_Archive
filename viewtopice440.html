10W UV380/UV390 PLUS - WORK IN PROGRESS

Post by VK3KYY » Tue Jan 09, 2024 8:37 pm

We have received multiple reports of the latest 10W UV380/390 "PLUS" versions not working correctly with the firmware.

It appears that initial / older 5W versions of the UV380/390 PLUS may work OK, but newer 10W radios do not work correctly

Unfortunately we do not know what hardware changes have been bade by the manufacturer (TYT) in newer version of this radio which make it incompatible.

We also do not know whether the PLUS version of the radio is still manufactured or whether the new production runs are only the 10W version

I have attempted to contact TYT on multiple occasions, directly and via dealers, over the last 6 months, but TYT are not willing to provide any technical information about their radios.



I do not personally own the PLUS versions of these radios, and do not have the time or money to buy one of them to attempt to understand why the new versions don't work. However its possible that someone who has bought one of the radios which does not seem to be compatible will be able to analyse why the new radios don't work.

The source code for the UV380 firmware is available for anyone who wants to modify the firmware to work with this new hardware


Edit.

We now made some progress to support the 10W UV380 PLUS version, but need more testers

Baofeng seem to have even less communication than TYT. The Baofeng do not seem to post firmware for many of their radios, so its not even possible to return these radios to the firmware which was originally in the radio when it was purchased

Its likely that both TYT and Baofeng are actually quasi-governmental companies, because they probably also produce radio hardware for the Chinese government etc

Its always been unclear who writes the firmware for the radios. It does not seem to be TYT or Baofeng and is likely to be another government department which writes the firmware, as we know the same organisation writes the firmware in both Baofeng and TYT radios, hence why its easy to Baofeng to make radios like the old DM-1801 and DM-1701 which are almost identical to the TYT radios and then instantly have firmware for these radios, becuase its just a modified version of the original source code from whoever writes the firmware for the TYT radios
