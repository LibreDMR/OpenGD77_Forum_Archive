Post by VK3KYY » Wed Nov 06, 2024 9:07 pm

If you can find a cable for a TYT radio e.g. UV380 or MD380 or a GD77 , you can open the plug and swap the data+ and data- wires

Or if you can find a Baofeng RD-5R / DM-5R they will work with no modifications

If the 'plug' has any chips in it, then its no good, you won't be able to modify it.
