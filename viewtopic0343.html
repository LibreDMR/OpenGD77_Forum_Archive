Hello community,

I have two TYT-MD9600 devices and problems with the GPS.

Of course, according to the original operating instructions,
I made all the settings for receiving the GPS signal.
But unfortunately the devices can neither find satellite nor GPS.
I documented the problem in a series of photos.
- Since I can only put 3 photos here, I have a link to several images here:
http://do1abc.vfkmarburg.de/index.php/p ... yt-md9600/

The GPS and satellite options are displayed in the menu, but no data is displayed.
I saw the GPS radio on an identical device, the Retevis-RT90, from a radio amateur friend of mine Why the GPS doesn't work on the TYT-MD9600 is absolutely unclear to me.
Have I perhaps forgotten something? Do I need a separate GPS antenna?
I also asked my questions in various forums and added pictures.
But nobody could help me.
The question came up in the forum as to whether I had even bought GPS versions.
Unfortunately I don't have any information about the original support from TYT.
For the original TYT-CPS I use the firmware "MD9600-CSV-GPS(2571V5)-P26.45.bin".
I also read various reports that the TYT-MD9600 often had problems with GPS and that the workmanship was not of good quality.
Yes, at least there is a GPS note on my devices and the boxes.
And to be on the safe side, I tried both variants.
Once with the manufacturer's original CPS and once with OpenGD77.
In both cases I found a subsection for GPS.
In the OpenGD77 software "Update firmware" is displayed to me. that I should use the donor firmware "MD9600-CSV(2571V5)-V26.45.bin". I noticed that there is no GPS noted in the file. To continue, I was informed that I should use the firmware "MD9600_firmware_V5HW.bin".
In OpenGD77 I had to activate GPS separately in the General menu.
But nothing happens.
Do you perhaps have any suggestions as to what else could be done?
