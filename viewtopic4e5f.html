in the official firmware?

with the radio off...

press and hold SK1 Key + Key 1 and turn on the radio, you will see Memory Reset? in the screen, and then confirm with Menu button and the led will flash in orange color for few seconds, and then it will be factory reset/hard reset.

in OpenGD77 firmware?

with the radio off...

press and hold the SK2 key and turn on the radio, you will see Settings Updated in the screen (that will reset settings only), and after that open the OpenGD77 CPS and write a new codeplug (that will reset the other things).
