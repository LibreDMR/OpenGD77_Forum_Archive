Hi Roger,

Thanks for explaining, now I understand the meaning of "donor software", it's just the AMBE codec stuff.

Adjusting the "Freq Adjust" setting for both VHF / UHF I had performed some days ago, but I probably missed to hit SK2 + "green" to save settings permanently. I've just repeated the calibration against my Racal Dana 1992 disciplined with Leo Bodnar's GPSDO, each band center freq was some dozen of Hz below the target, I've adjusted it again and saved it. Let's see in the next days, if there is any improvement audible.

In general, DM-1701 seems to perform somewhat better in analogue mode: It is pretty sensitive for a HT and it lacks of all this digital noise which other radio like Anytone or older DMR HTs show on analogue.

I hope development of OpenGD77 will proceed further in future, thanks for the already achieved milestones!
