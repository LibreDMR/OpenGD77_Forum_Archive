Success!!... here is what I did.
Downloaded and install the latest (OpenGD77CPSInstaller_R2025.02.03.01)
Using OpenGD77 CPS version R2025.02.03.01

Downloaded and used the 210525 MD-9600 V4 firmware for PLL2571 (as the doner firmware).

Downloaded and used the OpenMDUV380.zip firmware (not the 10 Watt or GPS edition).
Did the firmware installation and screen now boots to VFO mode.

VA2PPZ is now very happy

Or should I shay, back to 10Watts TX?
