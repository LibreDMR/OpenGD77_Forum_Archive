Problem hitting Retevis RT-97P repeater

Posted: Thu Apr 17, 2025 6:25 pm
by kj7gqa
I have a TYT MD-UV390 radio. I'm running the openGD77 firmware. Something weird is happening when I try to transmit to a Retevis RT-97P DMR repeater. When I transmit, the repeater doesn't see the transmission and it's not repeated. The repeater is in the same room as me.

However, if I transmit with a different radio, a Retevis RT29d, the repeater sees that. The screen on the repeater screen shows R-T slot 1 when receiving (and repeating) the transmission. And I receive the transmission on the md-UV390. For a few seconds after that, if I transmit with the MD-UV390, it works. But after a few seconds, the repeater no longer displays "R-T slot 1" and at that point I cannot transmit from the MD-UV390.

So clearly the radio is capable of transmitting to the repeater, but it only works for a short time after another radio transmits to it, and it hasn't closed out that session.

I have tried opengd77 stable version 20240908 as well as beta version 20250323.

I have programmed in my channel with the CPS, as well as directly to the radio in VFO mode and it acts the same.

Channel settings in the TYT with OpenGD77:
