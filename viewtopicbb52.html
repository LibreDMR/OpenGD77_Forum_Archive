Hello,

I have ordered a new TYT MD-9600 without GPS from this dealer:
https://www.passion-radio.com/mobile/md9600-505.html

OpenGD77 Firmware "MD9600_firmware_V5HW.zip" flashed.
Device works perfectly.

The squelch can be set in "FM" mode using the arrow keys. The squelch closes.
However, you can still hear a low noise in the loudspeaker when the squelch is closed.
In "DMR" mode, you can also hear a slight hiss, but a little less than in FM!

I have a second MD-9600 and when the squelch is closed there is absolutely no noise in the loudspeaker!

Can anyone here give me a tip on how to eliminate the low noise when the squelch is closed?

Post by G4EML » Wed Apr 02, 2025 9:51 pm

The older radios just had a MOSFET which shorted out the input to the audio amplifier to mute the signal. This worked fairly well but could cause clicks when the squelch opened and closed as it also affected the DC biasing of the amplifier chip.

Maybe they have changed the mute circuit to try to reduce the clicks at the expense of reduced audio attenuation.
