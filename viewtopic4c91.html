Post by G4EML » Mon May 23, 2022 9:20 am

As the two others have said the clue is that you mention pressing the power button.

To get the radio into boot loader mode you need to completely remove the power supply from the radio, either by turning off the power supply itself or by disconnecting the cable.
Then hold the two buttons and re-apply the power. You should not be pressing the power button on the radio.
