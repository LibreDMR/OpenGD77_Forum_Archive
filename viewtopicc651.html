by F1RMB
And you don't need to flash any original firmware before the OpenGD77, as it's a complete firmware written from scratch.

About the cable, as SA0BUX wrote, there is no chip inside. That means, if you plug the cable alone on a USB port, nothing will happen, no new device is added to the device manager.
