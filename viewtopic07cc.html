MD-9600 Receive Audio, how to improve?

  • Quote

Post by KD7MW » Mon Jun 17, 2024 6:14 am

Does anyone have recommendations on how to improve the received audio quality of a TYT MD-9600? I'm experiencing "listener fatigue." Has anyone improved the audio by replacing the internal speaker with a higher quality one? If so, what specific make/model of speaker?

Details: After a few month's absence from digital voice, I updated both my GD-77 and MD-9600 to the current beta firmware and did some listening. The GD77 just sounds better, despite its smaller size. And "riding the gain" is much easier. My Icom ID-31a Plus sounds the best of all, despite the D-Star codec's less stellar reputation.

On the TYT 9600, I long ago performed the mod that makes the volume control a little more linear, and also the hardware click suppression mod. Both mods were done using the recommended component values, and I didn't notice any appreciable difference in the audio quality. The TYT 9600 audio is "tubby." It attenuates higher frequency speech components, which are also distorted on audio peaks. This is made worse by the vastly different audio levels of many stations, which is out of our control. "Riding the gain" is often necessary. After the volume control mod, it's not so hair-trigger, but it still goes from inaudible to too loud in a very small amount of rotation. I've set the DMR Rx AGC option from Off to 0db, which helps a little.

Connecting an external speaker to the MD-9600 helps with the frequency response. The trick is finding one that's not so bass-responsive that we hear the PL tones on analog FM. It should emphasize mid and high frequency speech components, but not so much that it sounds like a tin can.

I'm also wondering if it would be helpful to put a simple an L-pad attenuator ahead of the speaker to reduce the audio, which would move useful range to higher settings of the volume control.

Thoughts?
73,
-Peter, KD7MW

 Interestingly, this is not an issue on analog FM receive.
The audio path for both DMR and FM goes through the same pot, so the rate of change of audio level, controlled by the pot, will be the same for both FM and DMR

There is also an option , in Sound options, to enable a form of audio AGC on DMR and set the default AGC gain level, which may improve things for you your specific case

Post by KD7MW » Wed Jul 03, 2024 8:02 am

Thanks, Roger. I added the L-Pad to the Motorola speaker, and it did indeed help. I dialed in just enough attenuation so the DMR audio's usable range was a little higher on the volume control. Now I have a much more usable setup, and it's far less hair-trigger than before. I am already using the AGC +0 setting.

On my 9600, analog FM audio comes up gradually as the volume control is advanced. But before adding the L-Pad, DMR audio came on suddenly, medium-loud, and a couple of degrees higher, it was painfully loud. Even now, for a given volume setting, DMR is always substantially louder.

I also discovered that the external speaker jack is not well-made. Just from inserting and removing a standard 3mm plug a few times, the internal speaker is now always muted. The contacts that disconnect the internal speaker appear to be bent enough that they no longer make contact when the plug is removed. And the connector is sealed in black plastic, so bending the contacts back into position won't be easy. I don't want to risk destroying the jack entirely, so I may just leave it alone. Since I want to use the Motorola speaker anyway, it's probably not worth disassembling the whole radio and removing the main board to replace the jack.
