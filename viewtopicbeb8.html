KA1CM wrote: ↑

 I've never experienced this, but I would suspect Talker Alias. Try turn it off and On and see if it help?
I found out on WPSD Discord this is how to turn off having the Hotspot transmit Talker Alias - I will test this out.

Under "General DMR Settings" in WPSD Configuration:

Set "DMR EmbeddedLCOnly: Enabled"
Also set "DMR DumpTAData: Disabled"

Description of settings:
DMR EmbeddedLCOnly: Turn ON to disable extended message support, including GPS and Talker Alias data. This can help reduce problems with some DMR Radios that do not support such features.

DMR DumpTAData: Turn ON to dump GPS and Talker Alias data to MMDVMHost log file.

I wrote a long time ago about the problem of sound distortion, as well as the complete loss of decoding and receiving a signal from a neighboring timeslot.
At first, they told me that they couldn't repeat these mistakes. Then they said that the MD380UV/RT3S has a hardware error and the factory firmware has it too. I specially flashed the factory firmware and made observations - I did not hear any sound distortion, but there were cases of silence in the presence of a DMR signal.
I may be wrong, but at one time in older firmware versions, the error of modulating was extremely rare, when the error became more intense, I find it difficult to say. It is quite problematic to flash old firmware versions with old CPS, they are already lost.
Also, these errors are only present in duplex. Everything is fine in the simplex. This is probably why not everyone can detect them.
