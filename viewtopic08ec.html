I have the Retevis RT3S with GPS. S/N: 2308R03S1104481 APR.2024.
In Firmware loader if I select either [MD9600-CSV-GPS(2571V5)-P26.45.bin] or [MD9600-GPS-REC(2571V5)-S26.45.bin] donor file, this message appears:
"The file you selected was NOT the official firmware version MD9600-CSV(2571V5)-V26.45.bin"
But in [operation.doc] it says:
Following two are for the version of 100.000 contacts
MD-9600(CSV-GPS)-P26.45.bin for GPS version
MD-9600(CSV)-V26.45.bin for common version
I am forced to continue with MD9600-CSV(2571V5)-V26.45.bin and [OpenMDUV380.zip] firmware.
The Firmware loader completes normally but in General Options the GPS is set to None and does not change when the [Select] knob is turned.
I have tried both the stable [R20240908] and the Beta version 2025 01 28 of [OpenMDUV380.zip] Firmware.
Any idea what is going on?
