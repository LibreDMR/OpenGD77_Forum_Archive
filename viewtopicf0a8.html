Post by F1RMB » Wed Feb 05, 2025 7:21 pm

The specs are quite clear.
It sends YOUR Tx frequency, the CSS (CTCSS or DCS), the shift.
The CSS letter could also be a 'T' (with 'off''). The case of the letter describe if bandwidth (upcase for 25kHz, lowcase for 12.5).
On the TH-D74, you have a "Tune" option when you browse the received beacons, that will do a QSY to that given frequency, with correct shift and tone.
Of course, here, there's a limit of the sent parameters to what the transceiver supports (and in case you are on a 1750Hz one, that tone is not sent in the beacon), range is also not supported, etc.
