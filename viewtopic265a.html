  • Quote

Post by AA3RP » Tue Mar 15, 2022 4:56 pm

I have an interesting situation with the Version 4.1 Hardware radio. I am unable to hear / receive the NOAA weather stations. I have a V5.0 hardware radio with the exact same code plug and it receives the NOAA stations perfectly. All the NOAA frequencies are set to Rx only.

Both radios work fine with all my 2m and 70cm repeaters and simplex on both analog and DMR. When I attempt to use the programed channel for any of the six NOAA frequencies on the V4.1 hardware radio the bar graph for reception never moves above the background noise level. Also if I turn squelch off all I hear is the noise floor. I have checked that channel has the correct NOAA frequency set.

If I go to VFO mode and input any of the NOAA frequencies still no reception UNTIL I PTT and then suddenly I am able to receive and the bar graph is showing a signal above noise level. When I power the radio down using the green power button and power back up it once again it is in the none functioning mode and I have to go to VFO and PTT briefly and it all starts working. I can go back to Channel mode and now receive the NOAA stations.

Below is the exact information from the PCB board in the v4.1 radio plus S/N and a listing of the six NOAA weather broadcast frequencies.

Serial # : 2006T00639

162.450
162.475
162.500
162.525

I am using the following Firmware (From the FW info screen)

Post by G4EML » Wed Mar 16, 2022 1:24 pm

On my V4 radio the problem was actually nothing to do with the frequencies. The radio did not receive on VHF on any frequency at initial power on.

This seems to be a problem with the VHF Receive synthesiser. It needs to be programmed immediately after the power is applied or it locks up and cannot be programmed until the power is cycled by going to Tx and back to Rx.

The firmware has a short delay after powering on the radio while things are initialised and this was causing the problem.

I have submitted a fix which should be included in the next firmware release.

Colin G4EML
