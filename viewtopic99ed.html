I am fairly sure I did that yesterday also, but somehow I missed the listing and ended up using the search function, which end up in my making the post. I apologize if I have created any undue concern.

I will leave it to you Roger, if you want to delete this whole posting or leave up as an example of how us humans are fallible even with the best of intentions.

The reason I was looking for the listing on the official website is because a supplier here in the US mentioned that it was taking longer to get them back in stock and they did not have a delivery date yet from TYT. The radio clubs I belong to have members that, bit by bit, are getting more and more interested in the mobile (MD-9600) I have and my ability to use it not only for amature radio, but also Search and Rescue with the Sheriff's department, and with the public FRS/GMRS and MURS here in the USA. When I mention it now does APRS along with Satellite they nearly do not believe it. So long story short, I find myself helping other club members one by one getting a MD-9600 and getting OpenGD on the radio and then pointed to the manual. As a side note, also a lot of Comet CA-2x4SR antennas are getting purchased, as they work great across the various different frequencies.

Thank you Roger and all that help you in creating and maintaining this fantastic Firmware across all the supported radios.
