Post by F1RMB » Wed Apr 16, 2025 3:04 pm

No.
You need and external progammer device to restore the MCU.

If you want to get back to manufacturer firmware, you need to restore the flash, then flash the manufacturer's firmware with the manufacturer's tools.

All of this is documented in the installation procedure, but as usual, (almost) no one reads the documentations.
