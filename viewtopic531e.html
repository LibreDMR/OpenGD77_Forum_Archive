I know this isn't particularly important but I figured I'd throw this out there just as a FYI.

I have a zone full of ham repeaters, hit scan and it stops on a ham repeater. The distance says "--- km". The radio is set to scan mode: hold and roaming is set to off. I turn the knob to the left to stop the scanning and the distance "xx.x km" appears (sometimes I have to turn left again to the previous channel and then turn right to get back on the channel with traffic for the distance appears - it's a little inconsistent). I hit scan again, then turn the knob to the right to move on scanning the rest of the zone. If the radio doesn't pick up any traffic on other channels and ends up stopping again on the same channel, the distance will appear, but if it stops on another active channel it sometimes shows the distance, but usually reverts back to "--- km". So basically I think what you are saying is that the distance is not showing during scaning, even when it holds on a channel.

I will need to look at the code, but if this is the case, I think what is probably happening is that the distance calculation is disabled during scanning, as its quite processor intensive and not necessary for it to calculate the distance multiple times per second as each channel is loaded and the RSSI/SN check to see if there is a signal.

But... If the scanning holds on a channel, or the operator exists from the scan, then the distance should be displayed, and it sounds like this is not happening.

I've had a look at this and its definitely a bug, but its a not quite what I thought you had described.

What appears to be happening, is if the scanning holds on a channel its not calculating the distance to that repeater, its displaying the last distance that was calculated to a repeater

e.g. If you start on Channel 1 and its distance is 10.5km and then start scanning. When the scan holds on a channel because of a signal, the distance that is displayed to that Channel is show as 10.5km even if the distance to the repeater is not 10.5km, i.e its using the cached distance

I will investigate and fix this problem
