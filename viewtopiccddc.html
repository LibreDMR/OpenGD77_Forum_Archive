Would anyone have a schematic of the CH1801 charger. My charger stays in the Charge mode. Never switches from RED to GREEN
indicating CHARGE COMPLETE. It's been that way from day one but just assumed I never charged long enough.
Charging it for more than 10 hours still doesn't change. The battery measures around 8.3 volts when removed
from the charger and seems to last a long time. I tried tracing the etch and came up with something schematic wise
that is 99% the same as a what's called a SC6038. The only difference is they run the EN pin through a 10K to the D.C
input instead of just connecting it directly to D.C. input. EN high turns the chip on. Would appreciate anyones experience with
the CH1801 and if a schematic is available just in case I missed something.

Guessing it's the HXN-WS chip is bad. Might try a TP5100 charger board in the near future.

n1kk wrote: ↑
Wed Jul 28, 2021 3:54 pm
 Would anyone have a schematic of the CH1801 charger. My charger stays in the Charge mode. Never switches from RED to GREEN
indicating CHARGE COMPLETE.
I have two of them that do exactly what you describe. When new, they worked just fine. (charging overnight, would be GREEN by morning)
Then one of them stayed red all the time when radio in the charger. THEN that charger stayed red all the time even when there was no radio in the charger.
Then, the 2nd one would show the GREEN, but very dimly. Now, the 2nd one shows NO color, but will charge the radio just fine.

At this point, I have just grown to ignore it, leave both radios on most days until battery warning, then back in charger for 8-10 hours, and all good. The batteries never get warm in the charger and have measured the charged battery at around 8.3 to 8.5 volts, which is 100 percent in my book and certainly 100 percent on the Open1801 indicators.

I don't think I'm gonna jack with it, but it's good to see someone has something to try for a repair or band-aid.
