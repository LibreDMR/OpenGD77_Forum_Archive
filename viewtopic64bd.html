Post by S58SMS » Mon Jan 06, 2025 7:50 pm

Hello, this is not connected to ras directly, but i believe to slot hold time. I have seen few reports of advanced users on this forum with same problem. You must be monitoring multi site frequency or capacity plus two site or something in this style - which constantly changes slots, even between two modulations. Ras is often persistand on those type of systems. It happens qso is missed sometimes. This happens in scan mode or without.

I have already asked developers, if there is possibility to make an option ts filter time to 1 second, like it was on older firmwares and i dont know the reason they have changed to to 2sec max, but itn this case its causing troubles.

I have two baofeng 1801, one with older fw - works perfectly, newer ones with filter time 2 sec misses qsos sometimes.

This is not only connected to non ham, the same is if you monitor duplex hotspots and want to monitor both slots fast. If you monitor both slots and some modulation stops on slot 1 for example (on second slot also some qso) it takes two seconds to start monitoring both slots and "open" slot 2 modulation. For those, who want to jump.constantly from one to another ts is this setting not convinient at all.

Please if you change option ts filter time to 1 sec or 0.5sec minimum, to test at least if this is the reason. I think that also effect scan speed, as older fw scans faster but very rarely misses qso, newer ones have much more problems.

I think this is a point that can be improved, as it worked better in the past.

I hope you understand my point.
