The safe power on option may be set. This prevents the radio from being accidentally turned on.

Hold SK1 while powering on in this condition.

You can then change the Safe Power On option in the general options menu.

Post by G4EML » Wed Jul 12, 2023 3:12 pm

Unfortunately you are confusing things by switching back and forward between OpenGD77 and the radioddity firmware. You can't do that without the chance of problems. You must always use the correct download program. For the .sgl files from radioddity you must use update.exe, for the .bin files from OpenGD77 you must the the OpenGD77 CPS.

When loading OpenGD77 and the radio said "Settings Update" you should press the green key to acknowledge and it should then work OK. If it still doesn't respond then it sounds like you may have a hardware problem.

Colin G4EML.

I would add à reminder here:
Never do à factory reset on GD-77/DM-1801/RD-5R, depending on the official (manufacturer) firmware, it will kill the protection bytes and turn the radio into something you cannot flash anymore unless you flash a modified bootloader using a hardware programmer.
