Boot splash text should be shown before PIN

  • Quote

Post by kt4lh » Wed Jun 25, 2025 11:15 pm

In the case that someone has set a PIN, but their radio has gotten lost, picked up, maybe knocked over at a hamfest, etc, and someone may be wanting to get it back to the owner if the boot screen were visible before the PIN they would be able to get in touch with the owner. Right now it just boots right into the PIN, and doesn't give any information on the owner. Of course the owner would need to put something on the boot text.

Can the boot screen/text be shown for a few seconds (perhaps interruptible like the boot music is) before the PIN is shown?

I'd think in that case they wouldn't put anything identifiable in the boot text, rather than write on their radio. Writing on radios is ugly and rubs off over time. The PIN isn't remotely secure, it's just to put up a barrier between kids or random users using your radio so it makes sense in a lot of cases to have it on. It just seems it'd make a lot more sense to show the boot text first.
