 Just received a MD9600 that was advertised as GPS enabled, but it is notably missing. Given this was an eBay purchase, and I can't be bothered with the hassle, I was thinking of just retrofitting one as this is going to be a truly mobile unit fitting in my car.

Looking at the front display PCB, there is clearly a space for an IC very similar to other ATGM336H with the ant. connector next to it. Does anybody happen to know if the ATGM336H IC is pin for pin compatible with the stock IC, or what the stock IC is, so I can add it in without having a bulky module that's liable to come loose from vibrations?
The official GPS module is an AliStar, but I don't know which specific model.

You would need to check the published schematic for the MD9600 and also the datasheet for the ATGM to determine if you wanted to risk directly fitting one in your radio, as any information posted here may be incorrect.

https://www.lcsc.com/datasheet/lcsc_dat ... C90770.pdf

The big downside with fitting any GPS directly to the PCB, is that the PCB does not provide backup battery power to the GPS module and every time you turn the radio on, it will need to do a Cold Start of the GPS, meaning it will take far far longer to get an intial GPS position.


Also, if you intend to fit a GPS module, then you should consider fitting a multi-constellation GNSS module, rather than the ATGM336 which only supports American GPS (and Japanaes QNSS on the American GPS band) and Chinese BeiDou

More modern GPS modules also support Galileo and GLONASS and in some cases SBAS and other constellations.

I am currently updating all my MD9600 to ublox M10 compatible GPS modules, however I am running my own modified version of the firmware to support them.
