When you say the joystick doesnt work, I thought initially you meant that you could not navigate any of the menus.

However, my guess is now that perhaps you mean changing TGs within a TG list.

I recommend you reset the settings in your DM1801 by booting while holding SK2 in case the problem is something to do with codeplug TG list positions held by the settings, and not something specific to the codeplug you read from your RT3S
