Satellite pass times off by 1 hour...

  • Quote

Post by n1evh » Tue Apr 01, 2025 7:48 pm

Since Feb.... I've spent some time in and out of the hospital. This is the first time
since the change to Daylight Savings Time in the USA that I've played around with
my TYT MD-380UV radio.

I went into the radio and changed the clock by 1 hour. I ran the Opengd77 CPS
and updated the satellite info. Now... when I go to the "Satellite" menu and it
predicts the satellites... but it seems like all of the predicted passes are off by
1 hour compared to the Gpredict

The radio is saying the next ISS pass starts at 15:37 (3:37pm)... by my Gpredict
says the next pass is at 16:37 (4:37pm). The radio is saying that the next PO-101
pass starts at 16:11 (4:11pnm)... but my Gpredict says the next pass is at 17:11
(5.11pm). I know my Gpredict is right as I heard an ISS pass on another radio
when Gpredict indicated it was in range.

When I first got this radio and put the OpenGD77 software into it... the radio and my
Gpredict software showed the satellite passes taking place at the same time. Like I
said... this is my first time playing around with the radio since we set the clocks
ahead 1 hour here in the USA.

Any suggestion as to what to look at so the radio displays the correct satellite pass
times???

I also seem to be having the same problem with my AnyTone HT (they recently
added satellite tracking to that radio in a recent firmware update). Need to
figure out why that radio is also off by one hour

Thanks,

Mike - N1EVH

The firmware does not adjust for daylight saving time.

You need to adjust the local time offset yourself, when entering or exiting DST, or always use UTC

Post by n1evh » Tue Apr 01, 2025 11:13 pm

I found the problem with both my TYT with Opengd77 and my AnyTone HT. I
had to go into the menus and change the Timezone offset from -5:00hrs to
-4:00hrs. When I did that... I had to change the radio's time again by 1
hour (wouldn't of had to do this id I didn't originally change the time by
1 hour)... and the satellite pass times were correct.

Now... I'll have to remember this in November when we set our clocks back
1 hour. I sure wish they would put an end to this stupid changing the clocks
1 hour twice a year.
