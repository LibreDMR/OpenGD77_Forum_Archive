 Well the old RepeaterBook export didn't include the APRS, lat, and log columns and the CPS used to ingest it just fine, I'm sure it won't be an issue with the new column on the end.

The change is to the Channels csv not the APRS.

The Lat/Long in the APRS is for operators who don't have a radio with GPS but operate in multiple fixed locations, so that they can set-up different APRS configs for each location and then use the Lat/Long associated with each APRS config.

The Channels Lat/Long is used to show the distance from a repeater and can also be used to sort the channels in a zone, (for display purposes).

Hence I can't see a use case for the RepeaterBook repeater location (Lat/Long) being used for the APRS configs
