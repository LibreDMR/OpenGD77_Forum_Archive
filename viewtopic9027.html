SUCCESS!

So, it seems that when I flashed my DM-1701 for OpenGD77, I could not get the flash software to work in Windows 11.

Rather than debug the problem, I just went to Windows 10 (I am exclusively Mac, but I use Parallels to run Windows 11, 10 and even XP for my wireless radio CPS programming that are not covered by CHIRP)! And it worked perfectly.

But I forgot this history and all of my failed programming attempts to change the Codeplug were in Windows 11.

Then I received a reply to one of my Reddit /DMR posts about flashing the DM-1701 for OpenGD77 and the reply reminded me that I had used Windows 10.

So I just went to my OpenGD77 CPS programming software in Windows 10 and the PAPA System codeplug installed perfectly and works like a charm.

Thank you to ALL who chimed in on this problem.
