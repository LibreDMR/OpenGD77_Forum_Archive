9V is much higher than the normal maximum voltage from the batteries, i.e max voltage when fully charged is usually 8.2V
Using 9V will cause the internal 5V and 3.3V voltage regulators to generate more heat than they are designed to dissipate.

Did you try using 8V or less, which is the normal operating voltage from batteries.?

Does it work at higher power levels using the external PSU?

Did you measure the Tx power ? i.e The calibration data in the RD5R is not normally accurate and your output power may be higher or lower than this value. i.e possibly much lower

Also. Is the 250mW setting the maximum stable power even when suppied from an external PSU?



Overall, it still appears that Hotspot mode is not stable on the RD5R in its normal operating configuation.
