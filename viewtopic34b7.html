Be aware that the firmware can't completely turn the radio off, it can only turn off the display and the RF chips etc, and put the CPU into a lower power mode.

However the radio will still consume around 50mA

These radios are not designed for your use case.

As I said previously, the firmware can't turn these radios completely off.

Even if we added a function to put the radio into standby at a specific battery level, the battery would continue to drain. This is especially true of the radios with colour screens.

If you need APRS and emergency communication, you should use two different radios
