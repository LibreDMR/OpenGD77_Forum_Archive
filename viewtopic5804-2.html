Bruce has already fixed the button issue, he is now talking about a display issue.

Unfortunately the display module will be hard to find. TYT themselves may be the only place you can get one. I have heard of them sending spare displays to people via their Facebook account, but I don't know what their official policy is.

Your only other option is to try to repair the broken cable by getting it into a position where it is working and then applying glue to secure it.
