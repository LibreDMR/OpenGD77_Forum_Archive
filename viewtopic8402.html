Too low voltage of the screen backlight, I bought RT82 and changed the display from UV390, you won't believe it but the UV390 screen is bright, I replaced two resistors with one 0603 10 ohm, it became brighter, but darker than RT82

I will add that after replacing the output transistor for the backlight with FMMT718 (there was no FMMT717 in my store), the screen became brighter than the RT82, previously they used FMMT717, why they changed it is unknown, perhaps the output resistors did not need to be changed, but I previously replaced them with 10 ohm 0603
