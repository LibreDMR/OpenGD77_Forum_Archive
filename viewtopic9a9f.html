I hope I have this in the right place.

I find myself moving my computers to Linux for many reasons. As such I am interested in finding away to keep using the OpenG77 firmware on the radios I have for it. I know there are threads about people who have found ways to do so using Linux, but when I go to search for them using the forum search function I get the message the the term "Linux" is to common and zero results are returned.

I understand that developers do not have the time to create a CPS for Linux, nor are they wanting to invest their time in doing so currently. Very understandable and reasonable. Yet there are people for one reason or another without a Windows computing system readily available, but will have Linux.

I would like to suggest creating a room for Linux users. Place a pinned post that makes it clear there is NO development support from the GD77 team, and the room is for those that have Linux. It should be a place to find or create working ways and methods to use the CPS and GD77 firmware on Linux, and those of us using Linux can share and dialogue with each other in reference to this wonderful Firmware that has been created and finding a home for it with Linux.

AA3RP
