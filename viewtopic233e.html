Post by G4EML » Wed Feb 26, 2025 10:59 am

If you are saying the numbers you press in the microphone are not correct then that is normally an indication of a microphone fault. Try cleaning and reseating the connecting cable. The microphone buttons are sent as varying voltages to the main unit. Any poor connection can cause this voltage to be incorrect.
