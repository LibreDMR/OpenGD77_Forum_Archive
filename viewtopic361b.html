New OpenGD77 CPS 2025 01 26

Posted: Sun Jan 26, 2025 9:14 am
by F1RMB
  • Add APRS "Beaconing Silently" flag: when it's ticked, the FSK tones won't be heard on the transceiver (no effect on the MD-9600/MD-2017, as it's always silent).
The installer can be downloaded from: https://drive.proton.me/urls/B8KHSHZP8C#UxBfPUKojJM1

NOTE: you need a firmware greater or equal to the 2025/01/26 to be able to use the Beacon Silently option.
