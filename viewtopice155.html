Post by ad2q » Mon Feb 24, 2025 8:50 pm

I have this problem occasionally. My radio is a MD-UV390 10W running OpenGD77 beta firmware. It has happened across all OpenGD77 firmware loads that I have done. Currently, I'm running the firmware from 2025-01-26. Most of the time the audio output is fine, as you mentioned, but every so often I get exactly that output as shown in your video.. Sometimes, I get partially intelligible output with the other part being corrupt. It sounds to me as if there is some sort of bit stream sync problem where it's offset synced.

As a test, I have used SDR Trunk to monitor my MMDVM Dual Hat output. SDR Trunk will decode the audio properly when the radio won't. Testing this way helps to verify that the audio decode problem isn't with the incoming audio from the Internet, which sometimes happens, or the MMDVM itself.

I haven't been able to isolate an issue which leads me to believe there's some sort of problem that we don't understand with the programming of the HR-C6000 DMR chip. I seem to recall one of the devs saying there's a bunch of initialization stuff that goes on with this chip that they don't know what it does. My guess is something in here and it's likely to be a real pain to find without a datasheet or someone disassembling the stock firmware and trying to figure out what is going on with the initialization sequence.

Some people have said they don't experience this at all which makes it even harder to find the time to try to identify a solution. For the rest of us, it's hard to say for sure what's going on.
