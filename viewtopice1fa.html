I had no opinion for extended UHF frequency range. But I do not recommend to extend VHF frequency down to 12xMHz for following reason.

1:The civilian aeronautics use 118-137MHz in AM. Since the RDA1846 does not support AM at all,it would be meaningless to use RDA1846 to receive 12x-137MHz.

2:Support TX in 12x-137MHz in FM would be meaningless,and might cause harmful interference to civilian aeronautics.

I recommend the author could reserve extended frequency in source code,but restrict to within 136-174MHz in binary F/W to prevent unintentional abuse.
