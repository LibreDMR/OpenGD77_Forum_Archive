by G4EML
The older radios just had a MOSFET which shorted out the input to the audio amplifier to mute the signal. This worked fairly well but could cause clicks when the squelch opened and closed as it also affected the DC biasing of the amplifier chip.

Maybe they have changed the mute circuit to try to reduce the clicks at the expense of reduced audio attenuation.
