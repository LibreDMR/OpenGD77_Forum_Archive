Yet another one of my patented dumb questions:

One of the many reasons I love OpenGD77 is that I use the exact same codeplug for both my GD77 and MD-9600. I noticed the other day with my MD-9600 that while on a DMR repeater the time slot said "cS2" which I thought was weird, but I looked it up in the manual and saw that was added at some point (I'm not good about keeping the firmware updated - I upgraded recently and must of picked up this new feature).

I should also add everything works fine, I'm just curious why I'm getting different behavior from the two devices. Firmware on the GD-77 is 20241215160229 and on the MD-9600 it's 20241212141958.
