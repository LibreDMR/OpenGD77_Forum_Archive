One additional very minor thing to mention, which I just noticed. I'm not sure if this happened in the previous versions of the firmware or not.

With the PTT lock enabled, if a digital transmission is being received and the PTT button is pushed, the transmitting station information (Call sign, name, location) no longer appear once the PTT lock alert leaves the screen (the channel name is displayed instead). If the channel is changed to another channel and back, this information reappears. If a new transmission starts being received, the information also is displayed again.

This also happens with the Keypad Lock.
