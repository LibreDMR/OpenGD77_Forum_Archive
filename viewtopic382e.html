The MD9600 don't support the 220MHz band at all, this is why it displays the OUT OF BAND message.

On top of that, and probably the root cause is, your codeplug is just all wrong, look at the screen, power, color code are crazy values.

Flash an OpenGD77 codeplug, that will fix the problem.
