Post by VK3KYY » Sun Jan 12, 2025 10:30 pm

DMR ID database is separate from Digital Contacts

This is the same on all most DMR Radios, not just OpenGD77

DMR ID database is only to show the callsign and name etc of the station being received (Rx)

Digital Contacts are used for Tx e.g. TG and Private Calls.

dj5bm wrote: ↑
Mon Jan 13, 2025 6:48 am
 @VK3KYY
Thanks for your answer, I have a lot of DMR transceivers in use and they are all displayed in the CPS in the ID list, which is also useful for editing a contact manually. Why doesn't that work in the Opengd77 CPS for the MD2017? Unfortunately, only the call sign is displayed in the transceiver, even with the setting 2 lines and 50??
It does display the full contact if the DMR ID database is successfuly loaded. Also, the lookup order matters (see https://github.com/LibreDMR/OpenGD77_Us ... e.md#order).

The codeplug itself supports only 1024 contact, with a 16 characters limit, that's why the DMR ID database is located elsewhere in the device flash memory, and not available in the CPS.
