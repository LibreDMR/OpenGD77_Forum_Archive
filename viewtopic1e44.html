What I did to achieve the result I think you want is:

i) Go to 'Extras", "Download callsign database" in the current (or correct for your firmware version if not the latest) OpenGD77 CPS.

ii) Enter 1,30,31,32,330 in the Region field.

iii) Select the fields you wish to have on your radio in the column filter area immediately above the table . Do not change the "Separator" value unless you have a specific need to do so.

iv) Without making any other changes, click on the "Download from" button immediately below the region field. This should download the required regional DMR IDs and populate the table with the fields you have selected..

v) In the "Data record length" box below and left of the table, select "50" from the drop down list.

vi) Connect and power on your radio, then click on the "Write to radio" button at the bottom right of the widow and wait patiently until it's finished - job done!

FYI - When I did this I had 129342 records returned for the region search 1,30,31,32,330 and 279142 if I left the region field blank - remember that just because you only go into USA based TGs it doesn't mean that only USA based operators will be on those groups.

Hope this helps you achieve what you want.
