Yes, the almanac last for longer so a longer battery life is still worth having. However it only helps with finding the satellites from cold. Modern multi channel receiver's are good at doing that even without the almanac.

The ephemeris data is needed for the actual position fix and that only lasts for up to 4 hours.

Thank you for all comments,

After trying with ML1220, ML920, and ML621 backup batteries. I have found out the warm boot on GP-02 will never happened beyond 4 hours. Based on Collins information, I believe this is because the module has to download the ephemeris data due to the expirations of the data it has. From my finding the ML621 is sufficient to back up the module for 4 hours. There is no need for a bigger battery.

One more thing, the "Battery" on the GP-02Kit is actually NOT a battery. It is a capacitor.

Hi, I used the GP-02Kit to add GPS to the GD77.
I connected the power control to the N/F pin of GP-02Kit.
To reduce the consumption, I connected the EN pin of the LP3992 to the N/F pin through a 560 ohm resistor. The total consuption is thus less than 0.6mA (through the MCU "shorted" 10k pull-up).
