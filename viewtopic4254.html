I'm just curious about how you managed to break the pin. There is no need to lift any of the pins, as although the pins are soldered to the PCB, the pads don't have any connections to them.

Also the adjacent pins i.e pin 100 is not used, so you can bridge to this pin for added strength, or to pin 98.

The normal method is to attach a small wire between 99 and 100 , bridging 99 and 100 to make a good physical connection, and the same on 97 and 98

But it sounds like you lifted 97 and 99


With the GPS, they are normally quite sensitive but cold start can take several minutes even outdoors. GPS's don't work well indoors
