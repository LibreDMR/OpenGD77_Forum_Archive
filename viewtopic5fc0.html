Post by VK3KYY » Wed Apr 08, 2020 12:16 am

In PiStar the value is the percentage of max power

100 = 100%
50 = 50 %

However in the OpenGD77 we ignore the value if its 100%, because the radio's 100% power is 5W and we don't want people accidentally running 5W

If you set 99% you will actually get 5W, as the OpenGD77 evaluates the 99% to the closest power setting to 99% of 5W, i.e 5W

Its not possible to control the OpenGD77 power by percentage e.g. to get 37% of 5W, because the PA drive vs output power is non-linear.
There is a quite complicated calculation to achieve the power setting steps, which uses the 1W and 5W calibration data points for the frequency you are transmitting on, and either does linear interpolation (between 1 and 5W), or uses a faction of the 1W power data point for values below 1W

This also relies on you actually calibrating your radio, because the factory calibration is wildly inaccurate.

If you don't calibrate your own radio using screen in the CPS, the power output is likely to be totally wrong.
