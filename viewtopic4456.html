How large is the duplex spacing ?

I've heard that you should have at least 5 MHz as the receiver part in the duplex hat otherwise will be swamped with RF from the transmitter
and have bad sensitivity.
This will affect 2 slot DMR as the hotspot and radio needs to synchronize the TS timing.

In Sweden we have only 432-438 MHz and the standard repeater shift is -2 MHz , and because of the bandplan it's not easy to get enough spacing.
I have a MMDVM_HS_Dual_Hat which is using 434.9375 TX and 432.9375 RX, this works fine with D-Star but not so good with DMR and I usually
get those TIMEOUT errors to as the sync is failing.
If I crank up the power on the OpenGD77 radios it usually work better but it's not 100% stable.

You can also get better results by separating the TX and RX antennas.
