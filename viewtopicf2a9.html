Post by KD7YCX » Sat Aug 17, 2024 11:39 pm

I have a TYT 9600 setup in hotspot mode on a high gain antenna (11.9db). This works great, people from all over the Phoenix Az area can join in on Brandmeister or TGIF. My concern is FCC station ID compliance. Yaesu and D-Star as far as I know embed the station operators call sign in the digital header. With DMR its the DMR ID that's in the header and that's not a government issued ID. My thought was to set my call sign as the talker alias but I have not been able to verify if that would meet compliance or not. Any feedback would be much appreciated.

BTW thanks for the amazing work on this firmware, it took the TYT9600 and UV390 from radios I'd probably just give away to the best radios I've ever owned.

73's

This is probably a question for a DMR specific forum in the USA where someone would know the legal aspects of running a hotspot in relation to FCC regulation.

Post by F1RMB » Tue Aug 20, 2024 5:31 am

You have to enable CW ID (part of the MMDVMHost configuration, so unrelated to the dashboard, e.g. raw Pi-Star can do that too). That will beacon your callsign.
There is no DMR feature like this, and CW beaconing is the thing that repeaters mostly use.
