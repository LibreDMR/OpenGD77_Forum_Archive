New MD-2017/RT-82 Beta version 2025 03 23.

  • Quote

Post by F1RMB » Sun Mar 23, 2025 5:57 am

  • Reapply CSS RX once the DTMF sequence has ended.
  • Take care of RD5R display buffer height (48 vs 64 on MK22 platforms).
  • Screengrabber Python script takes care of RD5R screen size too.
    • available at https://drive.proton.me/urls/K2TF5ZCQJ8#NhIcfp2oKy0R.
  • Add GPS support for RD5R platform.
    • NOTE: the RSSI screen doesn't display the sat's number, as there is no room for this on screen.
The firmwares are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/C3W0KRETB0#BVTApda3XVTK
The voice prompts are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/CT7Y3QE8CC#ivbPuzbV8ty1

NOTE: You need to use a CPS version greater or equal the the 2025/03/23. See the CPS section viewforum.php?f=12

For translators, the on-screen texts, voice prompts CSV are located at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/AFEPKF84F4#2ltiJXlnndiy
