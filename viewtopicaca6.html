I made a quick test on my newly arrived V5 radio which is still running factory firmware ( S126.061 ) by connecting demodulated output from
my Marconi TF2304 deviation meter to a frequency counter.
( Have never tried that before so I hope it's the right way to do it )

The tone was 1751 Hz and and I also noticed that the deviation was 2 kHz for a NFM channel and 4 kHz for FM, so it seems fine with factory
firmware.
I also compared with my V2 radio running latest OpenGD77 and 1750 Hz was within a Hz or so but the deviation was lower, NFM 1.5 kHz & 2 kHz on FM, the deviation on speech is lower than expected.

I will redo the tests when I moved over to OpenGD77 on the V5 radio.
