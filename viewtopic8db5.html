Re: MD-UV380/RT-3S/DM-1701/RT-84 Test version 2025 03 23

Posted: Fri Mar 28, 2025 10:16 pm
by DG1GMY
I experience these dropouts on a MD-9600 (v5 TYT CPU) almost daily, yet erratic. Device is always on latest beta/test version.

The audio will suddenly go mute while the S-Meter is still showing the continued signal. The TRX is mostly operated @ 100mW via a *duplex* hotspot.

A click on "ESC" or a quick channel/QRG or TG change and immedate return to the previous one (or TS change/override via P4 key on a MD-9600) usually brings back the audio. A slight resync delay may apply. Another dropout may follow shortly thereafter - or it may take hours to appear again.

It seems that dropouts may happen due to a strong peak in modulation (within a transmission) or (more frequently) right after a handover.

As it may help with further investigations here's my configuration compared to stardard settings:

QUICK MENU:
- Filter: None
- DMR Filter: None
- TS Filter: Off

OPTIONS:
- Filter time: 45s or 60s
- Brightness: 20 %
- Night bright: 10 %
- Timeout: 10s
- Info: Both
- Timezone: +1
- UTC: No
- Last Talker: 2s
- DMR beep: both
- RX beep: Talker
- Talker: Both
- DMR Rx AGC: 0db
- Click Suppr.: On

Some of these changes are obviously not related to DMR and/or shouldn't have any impact on the observed issue - yet I am listing all of them for reference.

Click Suppression is only for MD-9600//RT-90 , but something similar would be nice for MD-2017/RT82 too as it has an annoying click when you use the keyboard.
Click suppr
This setting is only available on the MD-9600 | RT-90 and controls the Click Suppression function.

Some models of MD-9600 | RT-90 emit a loud click when the audio amplifier is turned on. To reduce this, the Click Suppression function can be enabled, however a quiet click can still be heard.

To completely remove the click requires a hardware modification to the radio by the user.
