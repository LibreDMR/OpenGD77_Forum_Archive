BA7IQE wrote: ↑
Sun Sep 29, 2024 7:30 am
 Is it possible to reduce the space of the GPS log ?

e.g “ #define LOG_FLASH_16MB_MEM_SIZE ((2 * 1024 - 128) * 1024) ”
You can reduce this, but becuase you need 2Mb, then your best option is to remove the GPS logging functionality in your version, then use

#define LOG_FLASH_16MB_START_ADDRESS (14 * 1024 * 1024) // Last 2MB

as the address of your font

Remove

#define LOG_GPS_DATA 1

from applicationMain.h

You may also need to make other changes, but try this first
