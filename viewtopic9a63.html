DMR APRS Beaconing

Posted: Mon Feb 19, 2024 2:19 pm
by R9OBQ
Good afternoon.
I propose an idea how to implement dmr aprs beacon in In-Call GPS mode. It will not be correct, not right, but quite working. Just checked the idea, personal call to ***999 (in Russia 250999) is perfectly decoded by BM servers and sent to aprs network. It remains to force Opengd with the frequency of existing settings of "analog aprs" to make an automatic personal call to ***999 with silence (perhaps through the known registers HRC6000 can turn off the microphone or make it as silent as possible) for the duration necessary for the transmission of In-Call GPS data.

I think this would be highly disruptive to other users of the repeater.
