This forum is dedicated to the OpenGD77 firmware which does not support any form of encryption becuase its totally illegal for Ham radio use.

The OpenGD77 firmware will never include encryption because even to add it to the firmware would be a legal nightmare for the developers.

So you won't find the answer to that question here.
