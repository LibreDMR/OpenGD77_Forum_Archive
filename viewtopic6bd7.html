New MD-UV380/RT-3S/DM-1701/RT-84 Beta version 2025 03 16.

Post by F1RMB » Sun Mar 16, 2025 6:09 am

  • Disable "Use position" in VFOs, as it's not supported.
  • Rework 'g' height in 6x8 fonts.
  • Fix flash storage corruption on all STM32 based transceivers.
  • Hide volume notification on power off (on HAS_SOFT_VOLUME featured platforms).
  • Use double height font, if enabled, in uiPowerOff and Radio info screens.
  • Display a marker when a channel has ALL_SKIP or ZONE_SKIP flag set.
    • It takes care of the current selected zone (zone/All Channels).
    • Zone is displayed: '<zone name> Ch^<channel number>', instead of '<zone name> Ch:<channel number>',
    • When Roaming is enabled: '<zone name> ^ <distance>', instead of '<zone name> | <distance>'.
  • Last heard screen:
    • Handle Up/Down key repeat,
    • Toggles to detailed view on SK2 Down, not Long Down.
  • RD-5R bug: Fix Power and ColorCode self changing to random values (it was triggered when roaming was enabled).
  • Fix DMR AGC wrongly applied to Voice Prompts and Beeps.
  • Italian update from Fabio IZ2EIB.
The firmwares are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/SBV3GYK0K0#cY3zv5cTUhCI
The voice prompts are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/CT7Y3QE8CC#ivbPuzbV8ty1

NOTE: You need to use a CPS version greater or equal the the 2025/01/26. See the CPS section viewforum.php?f=12

For translators, the on-screen texts, voice prompts CSV are located at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/AFEPKF84F4#2ltiJXlnndiy
