New CPS 2024 03 30

Post by VK3KYY » Sat Mar 30, 2024 5:08 am

A new CPS is now available

See

downloads/PC_CPS/Latest

* Translation updates for: Czech, Spanish and Catalan
* Fixes for missing texts in all languages
* Updated Satellites data from David N9KT

Note. Satellites data now has the ISS SSTV frequency as Frequency 3 aka beacon.
Beacon frequencies have been added for AO-91, IO-86, and PO-101.
Reactivated satellites have been added.
Inactive satellites have been removed.

Thanks to Daniel F1RMB for his fixes and processing the translation updates
Thanks to the many translators.
