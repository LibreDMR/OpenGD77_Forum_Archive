Did you enter the firmware update mode and the display backlight was blinking?


TYT MD-9600 | Retevis RT-90
? Hold buttons P1 and orange button, while applying 12V power to the radio.
Note updating the OpenGD77 firmware to a newer version does not require the power to be removed and reconnected, just turn on the radio using the green / power button whilst holding P1 and orange will enter firmware update mode.
