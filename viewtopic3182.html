I googled for the error message you posted, and people first reported this in 2019 on other forums.

Speculation was that the problem was a mismatch between the official CPS version, and the official firmware version in the radio.

There is still the possibility that the problem is the data in the memory, but you are not the first person to reload the official firmware back onto a MD9600, and the usual symptom of incompatible data in the memory, is that the radio becomes locked via a PIN number, and we there is a known solution to this by editing the official CPS .ini config file

There are no instructions. Use it at your own risk...
