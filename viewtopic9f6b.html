Has anyone else been experiencing an issue with the MD-UV380, in which the radio does not keep time? I have three of these radios, and two of the three don't keep accurate time at all. I'll set the time properly (I also have my UTC setting and offset set correctly), and it displays correctly when I exit and go back into the time setting (so I know my changes took effect). If I turn off the radio and turn it back on, the time is correct. But if I turn it off for a greater amount of time (a day or so), the next time I go to use it, it's way off (by something like one day and seven hours...this varies). It's almost like the radio stopped tracking time while it was turned off. I'm using firmware 20250128, but this was happening on past versions as well.

N3JAS wrote: ↑
Fri Jan 31, 2025 9:15 pm
 Thanks for the assistance; it is appreciated.
The important thing is that the charge rate of the battery slow, so you need to leave the radio turned on for a very long time.

G4EML posted some calculations about how long it took to charge the battery from completely flat, and its well in excess of 24 hours , possibly 48 hours or more.

The radio needs to be turned on for the battery to charge. IMO this is a hardware design error, and the backup battery should probably be charged regardless of whether the radio is turned on or not, as it would not make any noticable difference to the main battery. But this would require another separate LDO voltage regulator, which would add 10 cents to the BOM and the manufacturers of these radios cost cut to the extreme, and IMO don't think beyond the end of their nose.
i.e Why on earth wouldn't they connect the Rx audio to a spare ADC pin on the CPU, this would be a zero cost change, and add a huge amount of flexibility to the radio etc
