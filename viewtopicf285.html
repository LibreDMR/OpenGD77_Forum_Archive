MD-9600 decode tone issue using only the 100hz tone

Posted: Wed Apr 30, 2025 5:26 am
by N4AMP
My TYT MD-9600 cannot receive voice from repeaters when a 100 Hz decode tone is active on the repeater and on my 9600. I have to leave the 100 hz decode tone “off” in order to hear the channel. This is true on both UHF and VHF FM repeaters. The 100 hz decode tone is not a problem on my UV-380 or Anytones. Other decode tones are functional on the 9600. A couple other owners have told me they’ve experienced the same thing.

The 9600 was preloaded with openGD77 so I don’t know if this happens with the OEM firmware.

This is not a showstopper. I just leave the decode tones off however it prevents me from filtering out unwanted QRM with the 100 Hz decode tone.

Has anyone else experienced this condition?
