Follow the TYT MD UV380 / Retevis RT-3S instructions

viewtopic.php?f=19&t=2380

Except select DM-1701/ RT-84 as the radio and use the DM-1701 firmware.
Note. At the moment becuase the DM-1701 and RT-84 share the same internal hardware the same firmware file is used on both DM-1701 and RT-84

However the plastic case of the DM-1701 and RT-84 have slightly different aperatures for the LCD display, so that on the DM-1701 the top 4 pixels of the display are obscured. So in the longer term there may need to be different firmware versions so that the DM-1701 uses less of the screen height, and will have one less menu item displayed.

Official 'donor' firmware is the same as the UV380

Hello, got last rt84 from Retevis yesterday - installed, works as expected with no problem. Some findings:
- as it has the same chip like rt3s, md380 - scan speed is much slower than on gd77/baofeng 1801
- the RX signal recieve is almost or maybe the same like baofeng 1801, good point
- more robust, heavier and bigger than rt3s or baofeng 1801

so in general it's mixture of rt3s and baofeng 1801 with some advantages and disadvantages, the biggest for me is still scan speed.
