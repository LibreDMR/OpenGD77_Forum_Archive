Post by NV2M » Sat Mar 29, 2025 1:09 am

Hello all, I'm looking to see if anyone has advice on troubleshooting my first openGD77 install. I have an old TYT MD-UV380.

I followed everything on the install guide on this board viewtopic.php?f=19&t=2380
Looking at my UV380 install I seem to have openGD77 build 20241215112654

The transmit side of my radio seems to have something wrong. I'm trying to discern if I have some configuration issue, or a radio/hardware issue. The analog side of Tx and Rx on both bands seems to work fine. I'm trying to get DMR working on UHF and the Tx side isn't working.

I have a pi-star 4.2.1 and an unmodified DM-1701 radio which both seem to work together just fine.

When I transmit from the unmodified 1701, the pi-start logs the DMR voice header and the UV380 get the Rx simplex. If I test the 1701 with a parrot service, the UV380 picks up the simplex and also the parrot response from the pi-start.

When I transmit on the UV380, I see the Rx led on my 1701 light up, but the simplex Rx doesn't come through. Also on my pi-star logging, it never receives/logs the DMR header or any other information. I tried playing with various settings on UV380 like color codes, time slot, power settings, etc. but still nothing. I have confirmed with an analog radio tuned to the same frequency that the UV380 is transmitting something that sounds like a DMR signal, but nothing seems to want to recognize it.

Now, the radio could be the issue here. I'm trying to find out if I did anything wrong on the openGD77 side (this is my first install) and if there is any way I can use my 1701 or pi-start to narrow down what the issue might be.

Thank you
