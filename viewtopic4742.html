Re: Can the MD-9600 receive U-U or U-V?

Posted: Thu May 08, 2025 6:16 am
by VK3KYY
It should be noted that although the MD9600 does have separate IF's for VHF and UHF, it only has 1 FM demodulator becasue the DMR / DSP chip is used to demodulate FM.

So I doubt it would be possible to demodulate signals from both bands at the same time, even if you changed the firmware to enable both the VHF and UHF Rx chains at the same time.

The firmware souce code is however available, so you could download, modifiy and recompile it, to conduct your own experiments in this area.

FYI.

Each IF has its own analogue RSSI signal level output, which is read by an ADC in the CPU, hence in theory the hardware is capable of true 'dual watch' if one channel is VHF and the other channel is UHF, however I doubt the official firmware uses this advantage of the hardware and most likely the official firmware constantly changes frequency when in dual watch mode.

I think most users would be confused if the 'dual watch' functionality behaved slighly differently of the 2 frequenices were on different bands, compared when both frequencies were on the same band.
