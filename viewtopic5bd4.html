Post by HA7VM » Sat Jan 25, 2025 1:21 pm

Hi,
I ordered a GPS module from Aliexpress (https://vi.aliexpress.com/item/1005001704030454.html), which is I think small enough to fit in the radio.
I tried to disassemble the radio but I had some problems.

On #1, I have the screws removed and I couldn't take the PCB out of the metal frame. After reading through this thread, I think the problem was that I need to desolder the pin shown at #2.

On #6, I want to ask if the pins are not soldered as they are not needed (it works fine btw), and is it safe to solder those to add extra stability for that potentiometer.

#4, #5 and #7 are just some representation of the quality... Not so good. On #5, the solder was broken and the picture shows the corrected one. On #4, the solders are ugly and I will correct those at the next disassembly. On #7, the screw was at this state upon opening, someone had a bad time assembling it.


(https://ibb.co/MB6Frbt)

The GPS module and its antenna:

(https://ibb.co/7rpKmsJ)

I already posted on this thread, but now I have the components and I have other questions too.

Multiple people mentioned that I would need a transistor to power the GPS module, as the pins can't provide enough current. However, I checked the datasheet of the MCU and it states the "absolute maximum current rating" of that pin is 25mA, which should be enough for the module (on the product page, it shows <25mA @3.3V).
I will check the current consumption later with a Raspberry Pi Pico connected, and my question is, is it safe to connect the module directly to the MCU without a transistor, if the peak consumption is indeed under 25mA.

I'm still not ready to do this mod, as I need some Kapton tape and thin wires. I'm writing to gather suggestions and wiseness beforehand.

Thanks in advance.
HA7VM
