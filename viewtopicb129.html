[BUG] Power issues with DM-1701 at 5W?

  • Quote

Post by MELERIX » Wed Mar 19, 2025 1:13 am

hello, currently I have in my hands a DM-1701 from a friend, and I have installed OpenGD77 on it (the current BETA 2025 03 16)

but I've noticed a strange behavior with power settings, for example in a UHF test repeater in 470 mhz, if I put the radio in 5W the radio TX never reach the repeater, but if I put the radio in 4W, 3W or 2W it reach the repeater without troubles.

currently I don't have a power meter but I've tested in my SDR in a random simplex frequency, let's say for example 471 in simplex, and if I put the radio in 5W the power in the spectrum looks is a bit low, but if I put 4W, 3W o 2W the power in the spectrum looks normal.

so I'm wondering maybe is a OpenGD77 issue? because with the factory stock firmware, the power is ok when is in High mode.

NOTE 1: in VHF it works normally, the issue is only in UHF mode.
NOTE 2: testing with a blank opengd77 codeplug or even resetting the settings does not make any difference.

I experienced a similar problem with my dm-1701 where I could not activate an FM repeater when using 5w, but could active using lower lower levels.

However the problem was not repeatable, i.e when I checked the power output using a power metre it was correct, and I was also able to activate the repeater.

My initial suspicion was that perhaps the output becomes distorted when using 5w, but as the problem was not repeatable, I could not do further test to try to understand why the problem happened at all

BTW. IMO this problem won't be related to the CPU type, and is more likely to be related to power setting and battery voltage
