Possiby something in the official codeplug , or other data in the Flash memory, in the radio, is causing the firmware to hang when booting, or possibly even enter a boot loop. i.e The firmware can't possibly check that everything in the codeplug or themes or DMR ID or voice prompt data is correct, as there isn't enough program space to add all of the necessary checks (and needless to say adding validity checks on every byte of data in the memory would cripple the usability of the firmware)

When the opengd77 is installed, try doing a settings reset, and then do a Safe Boot

If after doing either of these 2 things, the radio appears as the OpenGD77 COM port device, upload the same codeplug that you used successfully on your other radio, and the theme and also install voice prompts and the DMR ID database.

Is it à Plus or 10W model ?

About the reset:
- Flash the OpenGD77 firmware,
- Turn the power off
- Pull the battery,
- Wait 10/20 seconds then reinsert the battery,
- Press and hold down the SK2 button, and power on the radio.

If that doesn't work, pull the battery, power off, wait, reinsert and follow the procedure du boot in Safe mode.
