Hello everybody

I’ve been fiddling around with the radio to try and find outwhat causes the “silent DMR” and “helicoptering” sound issues.

This is the TL;DR:
  • Simplex transmissions do not have that problem. Setting your hotspot to simplex will fix the problem.
  • The issue seems to be rooted in the way OpenGD77 handles TimeSlots, and there are some wacky glitches you can reliably reproduce.
I posted details, explanations and anecdotal evidence over here: viewtopic.php?f=19&t=5001&start=20#p37673
