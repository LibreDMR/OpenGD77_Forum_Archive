GPS + BDS Module: ATGM336H-5N
Ceramic Antenna: 16*6*4mm

In addition to using the wiring method shown in the figure below,
02.jpg (238.5 KiB) Viewed 677 times

it is also possible to directly connect PA10 (MCU) and TX (module) without adding transistors and resistors.

BOTH the Module and Antenna are all set in the DM-1701 radio perfectly.
