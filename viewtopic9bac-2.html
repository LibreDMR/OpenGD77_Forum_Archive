There are several reasons why you may not be able to access a DMR repeater and are getting a timeout. Some may seem obvious but people are often caught out with simple errors.

The repeater is not operating or is not a DMR repeater. Check with the repeater owner or other locals to make sure it is working.

The signal to or from the repeater is not strong enough. Switch to FM and make sure that you can hear the repeaters characteristic buzzing sound. You may have to listen for some time if activity is low.

Your frequency settings are not correct. Double check your transmit and receive frequencies. Make sure they are not reversed.

The Colour code is incorrect. Make sure you channel is configured with the correct colour code for the repeater. You may be able to use the CC Scan in the quick menu to confirm this if the repeater is active. Or contact the repeater owner to confirm the details.

Your DMR ID is incorrectly set. Some repeaters will only respond to valid DMR IDs.

Errors in the Talkgroup or Timeslot settings will not normally result in a timeout. You may not be heard by anyone else over the network but most repeaters will repeat whatever you send locally.
