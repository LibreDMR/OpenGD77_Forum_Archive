Radio in DFU Mode Appeats as USB Mode

  • Quote

Post by W3ATN » Fri May 02, 2025 8:03 pm

I'm running Win11 (up to date) and the OGD77 CPS 2025.03.23.01 CPS. I'm trying to update the radio Firmware in my TYT MDUV390 GPS from 20240803 to 20240908 version. I put the radio in DFU mode but it appears in device manager as USB mode. Specifically, if I just turn on the radio, it appears in the Ports section as OpenGD77 (COM3) and I can upload and download to/from it. If I turn it off, put it in DFU mode, it then appears under Other Devices as a "Digital Radio in USB mode". So the CPS gives an error message saying no radio in DFU mode is attached.

This isn't the first update I've done and in the past so long as the CPS saw the radio in USB mode, once I put it in DFU mode, the CPS saw it as such and uploaded the firmware.

Any clues to a way to fix this?
