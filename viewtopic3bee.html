Post by G4EML » Wed Aug 11, 2021 1:38 pm

Those MMDVM error messages are nothing to do with the radio or its firmware, they are indicating a problem with your internet connection. The data is arriving too slowly and is resulting in lost data packets and hence lost speech.

It could be due to your internet connection or the server you are connected to. It might be worth choosing a different server.
