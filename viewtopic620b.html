DM-1701 deaf on DMR [Hardware problem]

  • Quote

Post by w9lw » Tue Apr 15, 2025 7:48 pm

Hello all, I acquired a DM-1701 last week. It worked fine with factory firmware. After I loaded the latest stable version of OpenGD77 (R20240908) the radio lost the ability to decode DMR audio from either of our local repeaters. One repeater is less than 3 air miles away and the other is less than 7 miles away. Both use Motorola hardware. One is networked to a c-Bridge and the other is connected to a Brandmeister server. The RSSI is at least 1/2 scale on both repeaters. The green LED lights but the radio does not decode audio.

The radio decodes DMR signals from my MMDVM hotspot without difficulty but of course that RF signal is much stronger than those of the repeaters.

An Anytone handheld on the same desk decodes audio from both repeaters without difficulty. When I reinstalled factory firmware, the DM-1701 was again able to decode repeater audio from the same location on my desk.

The DMR system is very critical, and with a small frequency deviation, this is possible. Use an SDR to adjust the frequency on the Baofeng.
I'm assuming you're using the original Baofeng antenna. If you use an external base antenna, the Baofeng could saturate, causing this error.
