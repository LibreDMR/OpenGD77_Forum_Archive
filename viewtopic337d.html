Post by G4EML » Wed Jul 26, 2023 6:58 pm

Try getting in touch with TYT customer service. They have in the past been good at supplying firmware.

The 026 at the beginning of the firmware version indicates it is for the V5 radio. TYT normally release three versions of each firmware.

006.xxx = V1 V2 and V3 radios
016.xxx = V4 radios
026.xxx = V5 radios.


The radio version numbers are what we use for OpenGD77, they may not be the same as those used by TYT.


Colin G4EML.
