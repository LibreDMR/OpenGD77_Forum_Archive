 Ok, so the steps are the same as installing the firmware for the first time.
Just upload the firmware to the radio, you don't need to do anything else. e.g. no backups .

Just download the firmware .zip file, go to the firmware loader in the CPS, select the language , put the radio into DFU mode, select the zip file and it will be installed into the radio

If you didn't update the firmware for a long time, the settings may be cleared because the format of the settings may have changed.
