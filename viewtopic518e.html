Re: USB Serial TNC with KISS on satellite APRS mode

  • Quote

Post by IU8RRL » Wed Mar 12, 2025 9:29 am

because it is more complicated for these reasons:

- You have to carefully manage the radio's microphone input, adapt the signal to the input range, remove the microphone's DC bias and fight against the RF couple on the microphone signal. This increases the possibility of failures during QSOs;
- If you unplug the microphone from the radio and connect it to a computer you cannot dynamically switch from APRS to FM mode (and vice versa) during satellite pass;
- Satellite mode has 3 options: FM, APRS and CW. If you want to operate with the sound-modem, for example on the ISS, you have to change and sacrifice the FM U/V transponder frequency with APRS V/V frequency;
- If you don't want to make changes on the satellite section you are forced to simply use the VFO without Doppler compensation.


...Anyway it could also be used on APRS frequencies to send more information automatically with additional hardware (like Raspberry Pi) and not necessarily for satellite activity.

If there are firmware limitations and you don't want to do that, that's fine!
I don't think it's so complicated that you won't get the right benefits from it.
But it's my opinion and I respect yours

Thanks for everything
