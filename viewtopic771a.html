Changes in this version

* On the Channel Details screen, the repeater Latitude and Longitude can be entered and also the "Use location" setting, can be set to Yes/No.
Note. the Use location is called "Location" because "Use location" won't fit on the screen
* Prevent Roaming channels re-sorting when a scan is ended.
* Updates to the Italian language translation

Thanks to Daniel F1RMB for his work on these changes and enhancements, and to IZ2EIB for the Italian language translation

The Tx and Rx tones are normally the same. It is rare for them to be different.

There is no way to passively detect the correct Tx tone. The only way would be to transmit with every tone until the repeater responded. Not a very frequency friendly thing to do.
