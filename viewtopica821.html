Possibly there is something non-standard with your radio.

Or you have some problem on your computer.

Did you ask the seller to install OpenGD77? i.e this will eliminate the possibility that the problem is your computer or PSU etc.


On rare occasions people have bought from suppliers who have custom commercial versions of various radios, and these may have a different bootloader which prevents non-official firmware being loaded.

The only way to install the firmware onto these non-standard radios is to directly connect to the hardware debug port pads on the PCB (SWD), and install a standard bootloader.

However I can only remember 1 person having one of these special radios, and I can't remember if it was a MD9600 /RT90 or a handheld

I am really embarrassed, but I wanted to write how I fixed the issue, so people can see this and try what I didn't

I did not have access to another computer to assuming this was a driver issue, I just deleted my disks and reinstalled everything in my mind making it equivalent to another computer as VK3KYY suggested. The issue did not go away and when I talked with the seller on how they managed to fix it they just told me that they flashed the original firmware and it worked. I thought they were not telling me how they did it since the Turkish language firmware is their licensed product and did not want to share the details with me. I decided to get a completely new computer just to try it before sending it in one more time, and it worked. Then I switched the USB port on my computer and it worked. My radio is to the left of my device where there is only a single USB port and since my MD 390 device flashed fine from that port I never tried unplugging and moving the radio to the right-hand side using the other ports.
