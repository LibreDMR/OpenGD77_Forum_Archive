Connect the gd77 to the phone with OTG cable, first press and hold the black side key when turning it on, this is important. change the channel to your access point, then, in TCPUART (transmission speed 115200), click on CONNECT, will the phone ask you with allow? click OK and then on the tcp server click SERVER, enter 2000 and START.
   press your start button (leaving TCPUART running in the background)
   start BLUEDV, configure, enable ser2net, enter ip 127.0.0.1 click send
   the radio should now say WAITING FOR TRACKING, click on the BLUEDV PHONE ICON and connect
   sometimes you may need to restart TCPUART
   If it doesn't work, verify that your phone is OTG compatible
   good luck

recent changes to the tcpuart android app have caused me issues with reliability with the gd77 as a hotspot on android (under windows its fine) so I looked at the video posted on youtube by Riku https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2OSyDwcrSuc and its dated Sep 16, 2019.

based on the date, the version of tcpuart would have been TCPUART_v1.2

so, I uninstalled v1.2.2 and reinstalled v1.2

using the latest bluedv 1.0.121 app

it still took 2 or 3 attempts to get it running, however, once running, it appears to be stable, however, on my old samsung sm-j320fn (Samsung Galaxy J3 2016) its a bit ropy , other, newer devices, work fine.
