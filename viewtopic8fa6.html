Post by OK2MOP » Mon Oct 02, 2023 8:01 am

1. Coordinates of the repeater or whatever else (in this case hotspot) the channel settings specifies
2. Distance to the channels with coordinates is displayed on the bottom line of the screen (if no distance is available, you will see --- km)
3. Does not have to matter, location can be set manually. Go to Radio Info menu, use down arrow 3 times and you can enter the latitude and longitude by numeric buttons, N/S can be changed by arrows and E/W by SK2(?) + arrows (RT3s). Once you confirm with green button, the coordinates of the radio are set and the distances will be calculated
4. For radio WITH GPS, either you can set the coordinates manually before getting the position, or the GPS position will overwrite them and distance will be calculated accordingly
