Hello all. I don't know how you guys accomplished this, but whatever I try, this GPS barely works at my place.
Factory, in the flat by the window, facing the GPS antenna towards the sky 0 satellites.
As above, but without the RT3s enclosure, so that the antenna is exposed: 2-3 satellites, no fix even after half an hour. On an outside window sill sometimes manages to catch a fix on 3 satellites but rarely. In an enclosure never .
Outside with exposed sky fix minimum 2 minutes 30 seconds and sometimes 10 minutes still no fix. Without the enclosure it works significantly better, then the first satellite appears very quickly, but it is very far from what you show on screens or videos. The maximum I have seen outside is 5 satellites and here you show screenshots with 16 satellites.
I bought these antennas on Amazon, but from the link https://www.amazon.pl/dp/B0BSFDHKFY, because the one previously given was not available, and I did the following tests:
- I replaced the antenna itself, without its pcb, being careful that the new one makes good ground contact on the Retevis PCB. - It works just as poorly as the factory one
- I replaced the amplifier with an AL5 - no improvement, same thing
Seeing no change, I bypassed the circuitry on the RT3s board and connected the second antenna from the set without changing it, as an active antenna, directly under the GPS module (pin 11 RF_IN via a capacitor what is to that pin on the RT3s PCB), and VCC via a 47nH choke from pin 14 (VCC_RF) - also no change, same thing.
Perhaps these GPS modules differ in sensitivity? My module is the Jumpstar JS-H210 F67. What else could I do?
