But this approach is self-censorship. We, shortwave radio operators, do not have to subject our amateur equipment to the homologation process - in most countries. Therefore, if a function is prohibited in a given country, it cannot be used (of course), and when it is not prohibited, its use is legal. This means that if you have a radio amateur license in most countries (I do not know all of them) you can have a radio station with any power, but you can only use the maximum power that is on your license ... and so, in one country it is 100W, in another 500W, and in yet another 1500W (e.g.) - this is what conscious use of the regulations is all about.

Please do not treat what I write as arguing, but as a free, friendly discussion/exchange of arguments.
