New GD-77/GD-77S/RD-5R/DM-1801 Beta version 2025 02 23.

Posted: Sun Feb 23, 2025 12:10 pm
by F1RMB
  • Add audio amp mute feature:
    • When the audio mute is enabled, the radio mode (FM/DMR) font is stroked.
    • Add "Mute: On/Off" in quick menus.
    • Use longpress '0' key for audio mute toggling.
  • Some internal cleanups.
The firmwares are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/NP5JFNAS1C#OaHOHeI1b5WJ
The voice prompts are available at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/D3JKT50X7M#Md1OQqCWViCJ

NOTE: You need to use a CPS version greater or equal the the 2025/01/26. See the CPS section viewforum.php?f=12

For translators, the on-screen texts, voice prompts CSV are located at: https://drive.proton.me/urls/K6NBA7FRH0#owtc56LE34hj
