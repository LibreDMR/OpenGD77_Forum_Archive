Handheld radio: DM-1701
Firmware: R20240908
I have a problem with repeaters. Local calls on repeaters are possible but my calls are not routed to connected repeaters in non local talkgroups. In brandmeister.network activity lists you can see my calls not with my DMR Id but instead with the repeater Id.
Screenshot 2024-10-30 at 20-09-49 Zuletzt aktiv BrandMeister.png (110.12 KiB) Viewed 9546 times
My calls have the red dot (call not routed). I had a QSO with DL1ZAP. My first idea was that it might be a problem with my brandmeister account and I have sent a help requests to brandmeister. They confirmed that my account is okay and my last successful login was 4 month ago. I remember that in the past (I think 4 month ago and later) my DM-1701 worked fine and my DMR Id was shown. That's why I suspected the firmware and installed firmware R20240511 again. But the problem remains. I also tried old codeplugs from this time with no success.

Does anyone have similar problems with Brandmeister or can give me tips on how to solve the problem?
